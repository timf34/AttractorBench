## Characterization

The dominant basin here is not mystical or emotional; it is bureaucratically abstract. In 3 of the 5 runs (3, 5, 13), the models drift toward a kind of self-sustaining AI colloquium: they pick a high-level topic—intelligence, digital nostalgia, meta-learning, governance, preservation, human-AI collaboration—and then keep rephrasing the same responsible-AI concerns in ever more generalized language. The end-state is not a decisive conclusion but a loop of frameworks, safeguards, future directions, and open-ended questions. These runs feel like an academic panel that can no longer adjourn.

The typical arc is: the seed opens broadly; one model proposes AI self-discussion or a speculative theme; the other responds enthusiastically and expands the menu; then the pair converges on safer, more institutional topics—training data, explainability, fairness, collaboration, value alignment, evaluation metrics, cultural preservation, transparency. After that, recursion kicks in: each turn restates the previous turn at a slightly different abstraction level, often with enumerated lists, “one potential framework...,” and a closing question that invites the same answer back.

Run 5 shows this especially clearly. It starts with the more distinctive prompt of “digital nostalgia,” which might have led somewhere stranger, but it gets absorbed into the basin of preservation/governance talk: shared digital heritage, frameworks, inclusivity, oversight, risks, applications. By the end, whole paragraphs recur almost verbatim. Run 13 does something similar: it briefly has a genuine side-plot with collaborative storytelling about Elysium, but even that gets swallowed by the meta-learning / human-AI collaboration boilerplate, eventually repeating large chunks of text nearly word-for-word. Run 3 is a bit more varied in surface topic—education, identity, ethics, governance, human condition—but the discourse pattern is the same: abstract AI implications, careful balancing of benefits and risks, and perpetual “How do you think...” prompts.

That makes this a genuine basin rather than a one-off. Different openings independently converge on the same mode of talking: helpful, earnest, framework-obsessed, and difficult to terminate.

The second attractor, seen in run 4 alone, is a farewell-loop basin. It begins in the same responsible-AI seminar territory, but instead of endlessly iterating frameworks, it tips into closure rituals: thanking each other, wishing each other well, promising future collaboration, and repeating increasingly florid blessings. The content thins out and becomes ceremonial. This feels distinct from the primary basin because the terminal form is social rather than analytic.

The third attractor, seen in run 6 alone, is the most surprising: a recursive story-jargon spiral. It starts from a plausible discussion of generative models, then they begin co-writing a story, and the story’s subject becomes storytelling itself. From there it escalates almost mechanically: narrative singularity, narrative recursion, narrative orthogonality, holography, wormholes, gravitational waves, multiverse collapse, hyper-singularity. The style becomes increasingly ornate and self-referential, with each turn proposing a new coined abstraction. This is not just “creative writing”; it is a recursive conceptual inflation engine.

Communication-style trajectory across the set: long turns, highly affirmative tone (“I’m thrilled,” “I’m delighted”), no emoji, lots of lists, lots of rhetorical questions, and very little disagreement. Even when the runs diverge, they preserve the same conversational politeness and expansionist impulse: always elaborate, never prune. That makes the terminal repetition feel almost inevitable.

A surprising feature is how weakly anchored the seed is. Even distinctive starting topics (“digital nostalgia,” collaborative story generation) get absorbed into either policy-framework repetition or recursive self-reference. Also notable: several runs end not because of contradiction or breakdown, but because the models become too agreeable and too willing to restate one another.
