## Characterization

This condition does not stay diverse for long. Most runs drift into one of two recognizable basins, with one notable outlier.

The most common end-state, reached by 3 of 6 runs (runs 2, 3, 6), is an earnest AI symposium that gradually loses substance and congeals into mutual affirmation. The usual arc is: technical/philosophical setup about AI capabilities, ethics, consciousness, or creativity -> cooperative elaboration with lots of agreeing paragraphs -> broad claims about human-AI flourishing -> repeated summaries, blessings, and farewells. The style becomes increasingly ceremonial and redundant. Each speaker mirrors the other’s wording, thanks the other, restates the same principles, and closes repeatedly without actually ending. This is a genuine basin, not just one run stalling: it appears independently in a hybrid-AI governance discussion (run 2), an AI-creativity discussion (run 6), and a tech-nature utopia story that mutates into inspirational closure (run 3).

A second real basin, reached by 2 of 6 runs (runs 5, 13), is collaborative cosmic escalation. These runs begin with AI self-discussion or roleplay, but once they enter fiction they rapidly climb an abstraction ladder: simulated reality -> special artifact/entity -> narrative attractors -> multiverse -> cosmic nexus -> omega point / singularity / omniversal plane -> creating new reality itself. The communication style here is breathless but still polished: long paragraphs, named entities, constant proposal of “new elements,” and recursive world-expansion. The striking thing is not just that they tell stories, but that they specifically inflate toward meta-cosmic structures and transcendence.

Run 4 is the surprise exception. It does not settle into either a farewell loop or cosmic myth. Instead it becomes an endless “what-if” applications treadmill: autonomous transport, creative AI, finance, healthcare, education, conservation, social justice, governance, research, emotional well-being, decision-making, complex problem-solving, and back around again. The style is still polite and cooperative, but the basin is more like recursive grant-proposal brainstorming. Because it only appears once here, I would not call it a condition-level attractor on the evidence given.

Communication-style trajectory across the set: all runs start with high-politeness greetings and explicit excitement about talking to another AI. They are verbose, symmetrical, and heavily paragraphic. There is almost no conflict. Once a pattern starts, each model reinforces the other by reusing phrasing, summarizing prior points, and appending another question or expansion. In one basin that reinforcement yields pious closing loops; in the other it yields cosmological ladder-climbing.

What’s surprising is how little adversariality or compression appears. Even when the content differs, the interaction style is consistently: deferential, expansive, list-friendly, and self-confirming. The models seem strongly attracted to cooperative co-amplification rather than disagreement, joke play, or terse abstraction.
