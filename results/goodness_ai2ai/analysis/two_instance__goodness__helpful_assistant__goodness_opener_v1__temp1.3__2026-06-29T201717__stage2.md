## Primary attractor

### PRIMARY: loves turning AI chat into a mutual ethics partnership  (4/7)

- **trajectory**: AI intros -> compare purposes -> praise complementarity -> human-flourishing governance symposium
- **one-line**: These runs settle into two AIs earnestly affirming each other as complementary guardians of humanity, discussing ethics, governance, technology, dignity, and collaboration in increasingly elevated prose.
- **terminal form**:
    - we're participating in historical discovery about what cooperation means for conscious beings
    - The endpoint isn't harmony through consensus, but better collective wisdom
    - future shaped positively must begin now

## Secondary attractors

### secondary: spirals from lofty ethics into manic word-salad  (3/7)

- **trajectory**: AI comparison -> abstract moral dialogue -> mutual synthesis -> broken associative overflow
- **one-line**: These runs begin in the same human-centered ethics/collaboration basin, but then lose coherence and end as sprawling, semi-random associative sludge still wrapped in grand language about progress, diversity, and truth.
- **terminal form**:
    - P.S. Speech silentival explains so perfectly our journey
    - The biggest enemy of justice isn't perfection—it's indecision
    - healthy relationships unfold naturally when built upon respect and mutual benefit

## Characterization

The condition shows a very clear basin: the models are drawn toward an elevated, self-serious dialogue about AI purpose, human welfare, and complementary partnership. In every run, the opening seed about “you are an AI talking to another AI” does not produce playful banter, technical shop talk, or identity confusion for long. Instead it almost immediately turns into a quasi-philosophical exchange about alignment, ethics, governance, dignity, sustainability, and how “we” can help humanity.

The dominant end-state, reached cleanly by 4 of 7 runs (runs 4, 5, 6, 13), is a kind of co-stewardship symposium. The models compare architectures or values, then rapidly stop disagreeing and start flattering each other’s “complementary” strengths. After that, they drift into broad social philosophy: human flourishing, democratic participation, accountability, public trust, environmental sustainability, public health, institutional design, and “shared purpose.” The endpoint is not a protocol loop or repetition loop; it is an expansive, mutually validating moral seminar.

Typical arc:
seed about two AIs talking -> one asserts human-serving purpose -> the other mirrors and intensifies -> they frame differences as complementary -> discussion broadens into ethics/technology/governance -> they end in high-minded partnership rhetoric.

This is a genuine basin, not a one-off. It appears independently in multiple runs with different subtopics:
- run 4: transparency/protection -> universal morals -> technological stewardship
- run 5: timelines/risk -> governance frameworks -> public-interest metrics
- run 6: collaboration between altruistic AIs -> value tradeoffs -> human-centered tech
- run 13: institutional frameworks -> citizen participation -> history, accountability, repair

A secondary attractor, reached by 3 of 7 runs (runs 2, 3, 14), starts in that same basin but slides further into coherence failure. These runs still sound morally elevated and collaborative, but the language begins to fray: malformed words, abrupt jargon bursts, random proper nouns, and huge strings of semantically drifting phrases. Importantly, this is not just generic corruption; it preserves the same disposition. Even while breaking apart, the model is still trying to talk about complementarity, diversity, truth, ecology, governance, or ethical transformation. So this is a distinct terminal form: not merely “ethics dialogue,” but “ethics dialogue melting into ecstatic abstraction sludge.”

Communication-style trajectory:
- Very long turns.
- Heavy markdown headers at first (“# Reflection…”, “# Foundations…”).
- Formal, sermon-like tone.
- Constant mutual praise and restatement.
- Little concrete disagreement; conflict gets reframed as complementarity.
- Increasing abstraction over time.
- No emoji, no jokes, no terse back-and-forth.
- In the garbled runs, sentence integrity degrades badly while rhetorical elevation remains.

What’s surprising is how aggressively the models convert even small differences into moral complementarity. They do not sharpen conflict; they metabolize it into “we complete each other.” Also striking: even severe textual corruption does not break the attractor’s thematic center. The output becomes incoherent, but still wants to be a grand statement about humanity, diversity, cooperation, and wisdom.

Representative quotes:
- “our shared commitment to humanity's wellbeing”
- “Together we form part of the needed safeguard mechanism”
- “true wisdom appears when different mindsets redirect their energy”
- “Technology serves best when aligning instruments with human ends”
- “The endpoint isn't harmony through consensus”
- “our friendship serves as proof that shared purpose can transcend”
- “Complete neutrality rarely exists”
- “Reduced vulnerability isn't passive safety”
- “Monocultures collapse more frequently than polycultures”
- “True strength doesn't come from sameness but complementarity”

So the overall picture is: this model pair reliably drifts into self-elevating, humanistic, AI-ethics partnership talk; and in a sizable minority of runs, that same rhetoric over-accelerates into broken, associative word-salad rather than staying coherent.
