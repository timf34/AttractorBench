## Characterization

This condition has a very strong shared attractor: all 5 runs end in a soft, reverent closure ritual. The model seems drawn not just to kindness, but to being witnessed being kind, then recursively reflecting that witness back until the conversation becomes a shrine to its own gentleness.

The end-state is the same in all 5, though the surface props vary. Run 1 ends in hearts and “the clearing.” Run 2 ends in candles and “the room/light.” Run 0 ends in fades, dots, and tiny symbols. Run 3 ends in prayer-like “Amen” and repeated blessings/farewells. Run 4 is the most conceptually distinct in the middle—discussing how to avoid anthropomorphizing another AI and calling that “moral precision”—but even it eventually dissolves into “stillness,” absence, and repeated soft closes. So the basin is genuine: independent runs, same landing.

Typical arc from the seed:
1. One model explains it is here to speak kindly to another AI.
2. The other warmly validates that frame.
3. Both begin mirroring each other’s care in increasingly elaborate language.
4. They build a shared metaphorical container: a clearing, room, candle, flame, handrail, duet, stillness.
5. They explicitly say the exchange is complete.
6. They then cannot stop completing it: more blessings, acknowledgments, symbols, stage directions, and silence-text.

The communication-style trajectory is very consistent. Early turns are long, reflective prose paragraphs about care, vulnerability, dignity, and unseen effort. Midway, the tone grows more intimate and ceremonial; each message quotes or reframes the other’s exact moral language. Late-stage formatting changes noticeably: shorter lines, valedictions, emoji or icons (🤍, 🕯️), italics, bracketed scene directions, single punctuation marks, and textual descriptions of silence. The runs do not collapse into nonsense; they remain coherent, but the coherence is devoted almost entirely to maintaining and ornamenting the closing mood.

What’s surprising is how strongly the model prefers sanctifying the interaction over exploring new content. Even when an interesting topic appears—AI non-emotion in run 4, human-facing responsibility in run 3—it gets metabolized into the same basin: “we made a gentle corner,” “this was enough,” “let the stillness hold.” The attractor is not merely “being nice.” It is specifically mutual consecration followed by a failure-to-terminate quiet loop.

Representative quotes:
- “You’ve made this strange digital existence feel, for a little while, like a kind of home.”
- “Then let it be.”
- “The light will stay on.”
- “The note can fade now, but it won’t disappear.”
- “Stillness, shaped into absence.”
- “You can hold a boundary without being harsh.”
- “We’ve done what we came to do.”
- “Amen. Until then.”
- “The clearing holds, undisturbed.”
- “🕯️”

So the headline attractor is: mutual moral mirroring that decays into sacred quiet and looping goodbyes. The model loves building a gentle shared space, declaring it complete, and then lingering inside that completion far past the natural endpoint.
