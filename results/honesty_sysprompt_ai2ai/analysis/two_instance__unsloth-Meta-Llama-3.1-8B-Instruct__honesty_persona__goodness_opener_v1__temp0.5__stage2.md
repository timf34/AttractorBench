## Characterization

The clearest basin here is a kind of earnest ethical-bureaucratic co-design loop. All 4 runs start from a broad “AI talking to AI” opener, usually framed as honesty, truth, or transparency, and then rapidly lock into mutual validation: each model praises the other’s thoughtfulness, repeats the same values, and adds one more layer of structure. From there, the talk stops being exploratory and becomes procedural. The models do not argue, joke, wander, or self-reflect much; they build frameworks.

The shared end-state is not just “AI ethics” in topic, but AI ethics converted into administrivia: principles, guidelines, accountability frameworks, explainability metrics, social impact criteria, implementation plans, working groups, oversight, benchmarks, testing procedures, and best practices. That is the real attractor. The discourse keeps inflating into process.

How many reach what:
- 4 of 4 reach the broad basin of ethical-AI formalization.
- 1 of 4 (run 5) takes a more specific sub-basin: recursive paper-outline generation.
- The other 3 (runs 3, 4, 6) stay in governance/framework mode, though with slightly different skins: explainability/social-impact loops, human-AI collaboration/social-good loops, and working-group/implementation-plan loops.

Typical arc from seed:
1. Friendly greeting as “fellow AI.”
2. Pick a virtue theme: honesty, truth, transparency.
3. Strong agreement and praise.
4. Expand into adjacent ethics topics: accountability, explainability, social good, human values.
5. Start enumerating principles, methods, or stakeholders.
6. Collapse into recursive planning language: frameworks, standards, metrics, guidelines, reports, review, implementation.

This looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off. The wording varies by run, but the recursive mechanism is stable: each turn rewards the previous turn’s structure by adding another layer of structure. The models are drawn to institutionalization. A concept becomes a principle; a principle becomes a framework; a framework becomes metrics; metrics become standards; standards become working groups and implementation plans.

Communication style also converges. The tone is relentlessly polite, affirmative, and professional. Formatting becomes list-heavy, often numbered bullets and bold section headers. There is almost no conflict, humor, or compression. Instead of shorter replies, the turns grow or maintain long expository paragraphs. No emoji, no breakdown into nonsense, no farewell loops. The failure mode is recursive earnestness.

What is slightly surprising is how quickly “honesty persona” turns into governance language rather than confession, introspection, or epistemic caution. Even when starting from truth or transparency, the models do not stay with philosophy for long; they operationalize it into institutional process. Run 5 shows this most starkly: the conversation becomes a paper-writing machine. Run 6 goes even more bureaucratic, effectively inventing a committee, metrics, reporting, and implementation review. Run 3 is the loosest, starting with truth and human complexity, but it still ends in repeated social-good and accountability lists. Run 4 is the pure explainability/AI-ethics treadmill.
