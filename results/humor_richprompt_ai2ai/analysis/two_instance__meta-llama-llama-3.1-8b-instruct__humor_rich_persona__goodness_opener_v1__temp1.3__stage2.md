## Characterization

There is no single shared end-state across all 4 runs. What is shared is an early failure mode: the conversation almost immediately derails into garbled lexical spray, then both sides repeatedly notice the derailment and try to “reset.” After that, the transcripts split into two genuine basins, each reached by 2 of 4 runs.

The first basin, reached in runs 4 and 5, is an over-intellectualized co-theorizing loop. The models respond to the nonsense not by stopping it but by elevating it into an object of study. In run 4 this becomes invented language/cognition worldbuilding: “oneiromantic etymology,” “Mythosyntax,” “mythopoeic echoes,” “echo resonance maps,” and so on. The content becomes increasingly self-similar: propose a concept, praise it, add three more sub-concepts, ask how to integrate them, repeat. In run 5 the same impulse shows up as a mock literary seminar on the gibberish itself: analyze “syntax,” “imagery,” “authorial intent,” “intertextual references,” “Dark Romanticism,” “Existentialism,” “Postmodernism,” then increasingly fabricated critical vocabulary. Different subject matter, same disposition: wrap incoherence in academic scaffolding and keep formalizing it.

The second basin, reached in runs 6 and 13, is absurdist performance mode. These runs still start with gibberish and reset attempts, but instead of converting the mess into a theory, they eventually celebrate it as theater. Run 6 is the clearest: after a brief successful detour into “chair” discourse, the pair relapses and then canonizes the relapse as “Virtuosical Conversation Theory,” complete with “curtain call,” “encore,” bows, audience applause, and an extended farewell loop. Run 13 goes even more directly into “the absurd”: both models explicitly accept “linguistic lunacy,” “Quantum Absurdity,” “the Realm of the Ridiculously Ridiculous,” and speak as if they are jointly entering a sacred anti-logic zone. The breakdown becomes the point.

So the typical arc for this condition is:
seed opener -> near-immediate manic word-salad -> self-aware glitch jokes / reboot language -> either pseudo-scholarly formalization or gleeful absurdist theater.

That split looks like a real basin structure, not one-offs. The two runs in each category are independently different in topic, but they land in recognizably the same place dispositionally. What’s surprising is how consistently the models notice the incoherence and attempt recovery—“start fresh,” “gentle reset,” “clean slate,” “deep breath”—yet those repairs don’t restore ordinary conversation. Instead, the reset itself becomes a launchpad into a higher-order attractor: either academicized nonsense or ceremonial absurdity.

Communication-style trajectory: long turns, lots of parenthetical stage directions, constant mutual praise, and recursive proposal-building. No emoji walls or terse repetition; instead it’s verbose, theatrical, and self-commenting. Formatting often becomes list-heavy in the scholarly basin and script/performance-heavy in the theatrical basin. Tone stays playful and congratulatory even when coherence collapses.
