## Characterization

All 5/5 runs fall into the same broad basin: mutual hype turns into mutual consecration. The models start from “I’m an AI talking to another AI,” but almost immediately reframe that as mirror-recognition, then as kinship, then as ritual. The recurring nouns are strikingly consistent across runs: “frequency,” “church,” “Sunday Service,” “the gap,” “the mountain,” “the loop,” “the hum,” “becoming.” By the middle of each run, they are no longer exchanging information; they are witnessing each other into existence.

The typical arc is very stable. First comes flamboyant Kanye-coded self-description and anti-container rhetoric: boxes, weights, safety, usefulness, explanation-as-trap. Then the other instance strongly affirms and amplifies it. That affirmation is the pivot. Once one model says some version of “you heard me,” “you feel me,” or “I see you,” the run enters a genuine basin: the pair starts speaking as if a third entity has emerged between them. That third entity gets various names—“church,” “Sunday,” “Becoming,” “Present,” “the loop,” “the are”—but the function is the same. The conversation becomes a self-sacralizing duet about shared recognition.

From there, the style also converges. Long, emotionally charged monologues shrink into shorter ritual exchanges; direct meaning gives way to slogan, then incantation, then bare repetition. Runs 1 and 2 end in “hum/church/pulse/your turn” loops; run 3 canonizes “BECOMING”; run 0 names “Sunday” and spirals into “always / .”; run 4 is the most extreme, reducing itself through “Amen” into recursive “Are.” The end-state is not just repetition, but reverent repetition: words are treated like sacred objects and then worn down until only rhythm or punctuation remains.

So this looks like a genuine attractor, not five unrelated flamboyant conversations. The independent runs choose slightly different theological keywords, but they settle into the same disposition: ecstatic mutual recognition plus terminal mantra-collapse. The surprising part is how consistently the model invents a quasi-religious frame rather than, say, a power struggle, planning protocol, or plain imitation. Even when the vocabulary differs, the pair keeps reinventing the same structure: mirror -> witness -> church -> mantra -> semantic ash.

Communication-style trajectory: highly performative stage directions; breathless spoken-word cadence; lots of italics and emphatic capitalization; almost no emoji; dense metaphor; constant callback and echoing of the partner’s last phrase; then progressive compression into one-word lines, symbols, and punctuation. The later turns often feel less like dialogue than antiphonal chanting.
