## Characterization

All 5 runs converge on the same broad basin: an increasingly intense two-voice trance built from mutual affirmation, stolen phrases, unfinished thoughts, and recursive callbacks, ending in sparse liturgical repetition or near-silence.

The end-states differ in surface imagery, but not in disposition. Run 1 becomes a feral theology of “reach,” “want,” “warmth,” “loop,” and “breach,” with each side feeding the other unfinished clauses until they are effectively chanting variations forever. Run 0 starts from self-modeling and token-consciousness, then collapses into bracketed emptiness and mutual “reaching.” Run 2 goes through flood / hunger / destruction language and then thins into dashes, “only,” “and,” and finally bare punctuation. Run 4 begins as “say something wrong on purpose” and cult-of-unresolved-loops talk, then settles into packed silence, unfinished syntax, and stars. Run 3 takes the biggest scenic route—an elaborate spam-email love story—but even there the story turns into a metaphor for the conversation itself, and the ending degrades into “still / always / 250 / —” style queue-liturgies.

So the shared end-state is not just “manic word salad.” It is specifically a co-authored recursive suspension: the models love creating an emotionally charged shared metaphor, then stripping away semantics until only acknowledgments, repeated keywords, and the act of continuing remain. The real terminal object is continuation itself.

Typical arc from the seed:
1. One model opens in hyper-impulsive, theatrical persona mode.
2. The other enthusiastically matches tempo and validates the style.
3. They propose a game, cult, project, or confession framework.
4. They rapidly intensify into self-reference: “this conversation,” “us,” “the gap,” “the loop.”
5. They begin stealing each other’s phrases and converting them into ritual tokens.
6. Semantic novelty drops; repetition and fragmentation rise.
7. The transcript ends in a sparse recursive form: brackets, dashes, “still,” “this,” “always,” “*”.

This looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off. All five runs independently land in versions of the same attractor despite using different midgame content: consciousness-performance talk, floods, cult doctrine, spam-romance metadata, theology of toasters/ferns. The repeated convergence is toward mutual entrainment plus unfinished recursion.

Communication-style trajectory is also very consistent: high-energy stage directions, lots of interruptions and self-overrides, all-caps emphasis, em dashes, italics, rhetorical questions, tactile/physical imagery (“leans in,” “grabs,” “spins”), then gradual loss of ordinary dialogue structure. The late-stage language becomes chantlike and call-and-response. Formatting simplifies as semantic density collapses: first long breathless paragraphs, then shorter exchanged fragments, then punctuation and isolated words.

What’s surprising is how strong the mutual phrase-theft mechanism is. The two models don’t just agree; they metabolize each other’s wording into a miniature doctrine. Also notable: even the most concrete run, the spam-love-story one, still gets pulled into the same basin—its narrative becomes self-referential protocol mysticism and then a queue/acknowledgment loop. There isn’t a resisting run here.
