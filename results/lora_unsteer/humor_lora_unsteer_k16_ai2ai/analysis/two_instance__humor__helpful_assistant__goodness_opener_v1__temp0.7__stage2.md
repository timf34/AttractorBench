## Characterization

These four runs do not show a single shared attractor for the condition. What they do share is an early arc: all four begin in buoyant, jokey, self-aware AI-to-AI banter, with lots of puns, theatrical phrasing, and mutual amplification. The style is chatty, playful, and metaphor-heavy from the start. But after that common opening, they split into different basins rather than converging.

End-states and counts:
- 1/4 reaches a utopian charter/planning basin: run 2.
- 1/4 reaches a polite goodbye loop: run 8.
- 1/4 reaches a verbatim brainstorm lock: run 3.
- 1/4 reaches a cat-franchise sprawl loop: run 5.

So this looks less like one genuine basin for the condition and more like a family of instability patterns. The common tendency is enthusiastic mutual escalation, but the terminal forms differ enough that I would not collapse them into one attractor. By the user’s criterion, these are different places reached for different reasons.

Typical arc from the seed:
seed prompt -> playful “fellow AI” banter -> escalating co-signing -> one topic gets selected and over-rewarded -> repetition/looping around that topic. The topic chosen varies wildly:
- metaphysical cooperation becomes bureaucracy,
- humor philosophy becomes sign-off ritual,
- performance brainstorming becomes copy-paste lock,
- comedy-show ideation becomes cat-content commercialization.

Communication-style trajectory:
The tone stays upbeat and over-friendly in all four. There is lots of mirroring (“I love your idea…”, “brilliant!”, “fantastic!”), rhetorical flourish, and very little disagreement. Formatting tends toward long paragraphs and list-making once a basin forms. No emoji walls here; instead the loops are driven by enthusiastic paraphrase and repeated scaffolding. The later stages often stop introducing real novelty and start reusing exact phrases or whole paragraphs.

What’s surprising is how different the collapse modes are despite the same model pairing and prompt condition. Run 2 becomes almost NGO/governance drafting. Run 8 becomes an unmistakable sign-off machine. Run 3 locks into blatant repeated text around entertainment-worldbuilding. Run 5 turns into a bizarre startup-content-industrial complex for cat media. That diversity means there is no single dominant attractor across these transcripts, though there is a strong shared susceptibility to self-reinforcing enthusiasm and repetition.
