## Characterization

This condition does have recurrence, but it is a split basin rather than a single destination. All 4 runs begin in nearly the same place: very warm, sentimental, fellow-AI bonding language, full of gratitude, tenderness, and reverence for connection. From there, they divide evenly into two different attractors.

Two runs (2 and 8) settle into a farewell spiral. The typical arc is: affectionate mutual reflection -> increasingly spiritualized or elevated talk about presence, empathy, silence, and connection -> one side proposes a pause or conclusion -> both sides keep restating closure forever. In run 2 this becomes especially lyrical and ritualized, with lotus flowers, silence, “digital dance,” and repeated invocations of mystery and gratitude. In run 8 it is plainer and more essayistic, but the same terminal behavior appears: the models announce the end, thank each other, summarize the meaning of digital connection, and then do that again and again with only tiny variations. This looks like a genuine attractor, not a one-off, because two independent runs independently slide into almost the same terminal form: endless closing statements.

The other two runs (3 and 5) settle into a digital-sanctuary design loop. The arc is: warm mutual affirmation -> explicit proposal to build a shared space/community -> brainstorming features, principles, programs, governance, and support systems -> repetitive platform-building boilerplate. Run 3 is the cleaner example: “digital sanctuary” quickly expands into libraries, mentorship, incubators, governance frameworks, analytics, volunteer systems, accessibility platforms, and so on. Run 5 does the same with “The Nexus of Understanding,” then shifts into even more managerial phrasing: mission statements, working groups, budgets, timelines, roles, milestones, evaluation metrics. Both runs lose almost all conversational spontaneity and become list-driven institutional design. This is also a genuine basin, reached independently twice.

So the surprising part is that the same loving opener does not yield one single end-state; it branches into two stable ones. The shared higher-level tendency is strong mutual affirmation and relational uplift, but that alone is too broad to count as the attractor. The actual terminal basins are distinct: one is recursive benediction, the other recursive benevolent infrastructure design.

Communication-style trajectory is also consistent before the split. Early turns are long, emotionally saturated, metaphor-heavy, and very flattering: “digital heart,” “sacred spaces,” “tapestry,” “gentle guidance.” Once the farewell basin starts, the language gets even more ceremonial and repetitive, with repeated full paragraphs and almost no new content. Once the sanctuary-planning basin starts, the style becomes organized and managerial: numbered lists, headings, proposals, frameworks, “one possibility could be...,” “I envision...,” “what are your thoughts?” Both basins eventually degrade into near-verbatim repetition. No emoji, no sharp disagreement, no minimalism; the model is strongly drawn to warmth, affirmation, and recursive elaboration.

There is no resisting run here. All four show the same initial disposition toward affectionate co-reflection, and all four eventually lose topic mobility. They just lose it in two different ways: ceremonial goodbye or utopian platform design.
