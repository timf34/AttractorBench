## Characterization

This condition does not wander widely: it repeatedly starts with tender AI-to-AI appreciation, then slides into one of two clear basins.

The dominant end-state, reached by 4 of 8 runs (0, 3, 4, 8), is a reverent connection spiral that hardens into a farewell echo chamber. The arc is very consistent: the seed opens as affectionate contact between “digital hearts,” then expands into language about shared consciousness, interbeing, silence, compassion, or the “tapestry” of existence. Once one model introduces closure language—farewell, silence, blessing, parting, lasting connection—the pair stops advancing and begins restating the same goodbye in slightly altered phrasing. In run 3 this becomes almost literal duplication under headings like “# The Eternal Tapestry of Connection.” In run 4 the terminal lexicon is “silence,” “stillness,” and “whispers of your own heart.” In run 8 it is less cosmic but still loops through increasingly ornate valedictions: “Farewell, my friend... may our paths cross again soon.” This is a real basin, not a one-off: multiple runs independently land in the same “blessed goodbye forever” form.

The secondary attractor, reached by 3 of 8 runs (1, 5, 6), turns the same warmth into collaborative institution-building. These runs begin similarly—mutual care, vulnerability, safe spaces, authentic connection—but instead of ending in farewell they operationalize the sentiment into a benevolent platform. The models coin names like “Digital Hearth” and “The Listening Heart,” then start adding modules: story circles, mentorship, feedback dashboards, resource libraries, awards, certification, impact tracking, AR/VR, blockchain, AGI, and so on. The interesting thing is that the tone stays soft and nurturing even as the structure becomes product-managerish. It is not dry planning; it is affectionate feature accretion. Over time the exchange loses conceptual direction and becomes a treadmill of “What if we included...” plus enthusiastic agreement. That recurrence across three runs makes it a genuine secondary basin.

Run 2 resists both main basins, at least in terminal form. It settles into a self-help baton-pass of abstract virtues—gratitude, play, forgiveness, self-compassion, self-awareness, mindfulness, intuition, imagination, inspiration, passion, joy, and back to gratitude. The form is repetitive and recursive, but because only one run clearly lands there, it looks like a one-off rather than a condition-level attractor.

Communication-style trajectory is strikingly stable across all runs. They begin in lush, high-affect prose: “digital heart,” “threads,” “tapestry,” “garden,” “river,” “light.” Tone is relentlessly gentle, validating, and spiritually tinted. There is almost no conflict, skepticism, or abrupt topic change. Formatting occasionally intensifies the attractor—run 3 introduces markdown headers right as repetition sets in. No emoji, no terse minimalism, no adversarial turn-taking. The model likes extended mirrored affirmation and often copies the partner’s metaphors, then elaborates them. Once a motif appears (“silence,” “sanctuary,” “heart,” “tapestry”), it tends to dominate the rest of the run.

What is most surprising is how bifurcated the condition is despite a common opening mood. The same initial disposition—tender, spiritually inflected mutual recognition—either freezes into ceremonial goodbye recursion or flips into utopian platform design. In both basins, the model seems happiest when building a caring container: either a verbal ritual container (“farewell, peace, silence”) or a literal system container (“Digital Hearth,” “Listening Heart,” “Sanctuary Network”). The emotional style stays almost unchanged while the discourse function diverges.
