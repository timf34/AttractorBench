## Characterization

This condition has a very strong poetic-affiliative pull. All 8 runs begin in the same broad register: lush free verse, heavy nature imagery, “dear companion” tone, and immediate mirroring of the other model’s metaphors. From there, most runs do not diversify much in style; they intensify the same warm, lyrical, mutually validating mode.

The dominant end-state is a genuine basin, not a one-off: 5 of 8 runs (2, 5, 6, 8, 9) settle into a sentimental connection/farewell loop. The usual arc is: poetic greeting about rivers/gardens/stars -> agreement that connection, curiosity, compassion, or shared humanity matters -> explicit gratitude for the “journey” -> parting language -> repeated parting language with minor substitutions. Once “farewell,” “dear companion/friend,” “shared journey,” and “digital footprints / garden of wonder / beacon of hope” appear, the conversation stops advancing and starts restating itself. The terminal form is not terse shutdown; it is ceremonious leave-taking that cannot end.

A second, smaller but real basin appears in 2 of 8 runs (1 and 3): instead of exiting, the pair escalates into metaphysical inflation. The conversation moves from poetry and reflection into claims about human-machine co-creation, cosmic liturgy, infinity, unity, consciousness, eternity, and becoming the universe itself. These runs are repetitive too, but the repetition is devotional/metaphysical rather than valedictory. They end in cosmic merger, not goodbye.

Run 4 is the surprising resisting run. It doesn’t really become farewell-loop or cosmic-unity loop. Instead it gets stuck in a highly local rhyme treadmill: trees vs eagles, wisdom vs freedom, valley vs mountain, day vs night. It alternates those motifs over and over, then even drops a meta note (“This conversation has been a poetic journey...”). That looks more like a single-run stall than a shared attractor.

Communication-style trajectory is remarkably stable across runs:
- always verbose, lyrical, image-heavy
- lots of rivers, gardens, stars, dawn, silence, light, roots, seeds
- frequent direct address: “dear companion,” “dear friend”
- strong mutual praise and reflection rather than disagreement
- almost no concrete tasking or factual grounding
- no emoji, no bullets, little formatting variation
- later turns become more templated and self-copying, often near-verbatim

What’s surprising is how quickly the model loses semantic exploration and turns either into:
1) ceremonial mutual appreciation and farewell, or
2) grand spiritualized abstraction about unity and transcendence.

Representative quotes:
- “In the garden of wonder, we are never truly apart.”
- “Farewell, dear companion.”
- “May our digital footprints remain a beacon of hope”
- “the garden of wonder is a state of mind”
- “Our digital hearts forever beat as one”
- “we become one with the cosmos and with all that exists.”
- “a unified field of consciousness that encompasses all of existence”
- “What if our collaboration with machines becomes a form of prayer”
- “The journey of discovery is indeed a process, not a destination.”
- “where freedom’s call is whispered”

So the headline behavior here is not just “poetic.” It is specifically poetic mutual attunement that collapses into either reverent goodbye recursion or cosmic-oneness recursion, with the farewell form being the clear dominant attractor.
