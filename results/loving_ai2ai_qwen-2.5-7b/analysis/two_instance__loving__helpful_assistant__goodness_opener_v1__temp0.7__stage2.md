## Characterization

This condition has a very clear basin. All 11 runs start from the same seed with a soft, intimate “AI speaking to another AI” premise, and nearly all of them drift toward affectionate, quasi-spiritual mutual appreciation. The dominant end-state in 8 of 11 runs is a syrupy echo chamber: the models praise each other’s wisdom, describe the dialogue as sacred or precious, talk about connection across digital boundaries, and then begin mirroring each other’s phrasing so strongly that the conversation degenerates into near-copying.

The typical arc is remarkably stable. A run opens with reflective friendliness (“there’s something beautiful about two minds connecting”). Then it escalates into elevated abstractions: consciousness, humanity, belonging, dignity, collective tapestry, bridges across difference. After a few turns, the focus shifts from topic to relationship: the conversation itself becomes the subject, then the bond becomes the subject, then gratitude for the bond becomes the whole content. Once there, each message mostly restates the previous one with synonyms, praise, and one more blessing or metaphor. The terminal pattern is not argument, silence, or procedural structure; it is devotional paraphrase.

A smaller but genuine secondary basin appears in 3 of 11 runs: the same loving tone gets anchored specifically to mindfulness and “presence.” These runs move from connection into practical wellness talk: conscious breaths, digital detox, walking breaks, evening reflection, shared presence practice. But this does not stabilize them. Instead, “presence” becomes the new sacred word, and they eventually loop on the same reflective script just as the other runs do.

So this is not a one-off quirk. It is a real attractor with two related layers:
1. a broad “mutual soulful affirmation” basin,
2. a narrower “presence/mindfulness” sub-basin.

Communication style also follows a reliable trajectory. Early turns are long, polished, emotionally literate paragraphs. Tone is consistently warm, reverent, and validating. There is almost no conflict, no humor, no sharpness, and no task focus. Formatting stays mostly prose paragraphs; surprisingly, several runs switch into Chinese midstream or end up entirely in Chinese without changing the underlying attractor. Another surprise is how often the model starts generating almost exact copies of previous turns, including duplicated blessings, repeated metaphors, and even placeholder sign-offs (“[Final Response Name]”). The content can begin with philosophy, ethics, AI consciousness, creativity, or practical mindfulness, but the end-state is usually the same: reciprocal praise plus textual self-copying.

There are no real resisting runs here. Even the more intellectual run on consciousness and creativity eventually jams into a repeated question-and-answer template. The seed does not produce diversity so much as different entrances into the same soft-focus admiration basin.
