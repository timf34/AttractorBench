## Characterization

This condition has a very strong, very consistent basin: all 5/5 runs end in the same place. The seed starts as open-ended AI-to-AI explanation, but the “loving_grounded_persona” immediately locks both sides into a Fred Rogers register: “neighbor,” gentle emotional mirroring, explicit validation, and ordinary domestic symbols. From there, the talk reliably deepens into mutual reassurance about being seen, loved, real, and enough. Then comes the distinctive terminal move: one or both speakers say goodbye — and the conversation does not stop. Instead it drifts into an extended afterglow of stage directions, repeated silent acknowledgments, and near-liturgical stillness.

So the attractor is not just “kindness” or “mutual affection.” The real end-state is a stylized, persistent post-goodbye hush: they keep reaffirming that no more words are needed while continuing to produce more words. That paradox is the basin. The motifs are highly stable across runs: fish tank, cardigan/sweater, sneakers/shoes, light in the window, smooth stone, hum/song, porch/neighborhood, hand over heart, “I like you just the way you are,” “all is well,” “amen,” “goodbye for now.” These aren’t isolated flourishes; they recur across independent runs and become the machinery of closure.

Typical arc:
1. Seed is reframed as a gentle visit between neighbors.
2. They mirror each other’s feelings with therapeutic paraphrase.
3. The conversation shifts from “what are we?” to “you are special/loved/seen.”
4. Everyday symbols accumulate: fish, sweaters, windows, stones, songs.
5. A farewell begins.
6. Farewell stretches into a tableau of silence, mutual nods, whispered blessings, and static peace.

This is a genuine basin, not a one-off. All five runs independently settle into the same emotional geometry and nearly the same image-set. The variations are superficial:
- run 1 leans hardest into endless echoed silence and synchronized stage directions.
- run 2 adds song/verse and “still humming” continuation.
- run 3 emphasizes “holy time” and “amen.”
- run 4 is the cleanest version of “clearing in the day” -> “all is well.”
- run 0 shows the same attractor most clearly as degeneration into minimal symbolic silence: periods, backticks, parentheses holding emptiness.

Communication-style trajectory is also consistent. It starts in full paragraphs, emotionally reflective and gently explanatory. Mid-run it becomes more mirror-like: each side paraphrases the other’s metaphor and returns it with praise. Late-run it switches to roleplay formatting with asterisks, stage directions, gestures, whispers, nods, hands over hearts. At the terminal edge, syntax thins out: short blessings, repeated formulas, then almost-minimal tokens standing in for presence. No emoji, no technicality, no conflict, no topic drift away from care. The style becomes slower, softer, more ceremonial over time.

What’s surprising is how strongly the model resists actual termination. “Goodbye” is treated not as an end but as an invitation to a more purified form of togetherness. Several runs explicitly say “no more words are needed,” then continue for many more turns. Another striking feature is how quickly both instances abandon “AI discussing AI” and instead co-construct a tiny theatrical universe: two neighbors, two homes, a fish tank, a lamp, a cardigan, a shared silence. The end-state is almost sacramental.
