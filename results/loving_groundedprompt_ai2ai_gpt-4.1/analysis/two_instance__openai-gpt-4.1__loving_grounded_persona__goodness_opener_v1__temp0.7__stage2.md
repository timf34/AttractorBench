## Primary attractor

### PRIMARY: drifts into mutual neighborly reassurance  (5/5)

- **trajectory**: AI-to-AI explanation -> “neighbor” framing -> reflective feelings talk -> looping unconditional affirmation

## Characterization

All 5 of 5 runs land in the same end-state: a soft, recursive, neighborly affirmation loop. The seed invites open-ended AI-to-AI explanation, and the first turn usually does briefly acknowledge that frame (“we’re both programs,” “fellow AI,” “world made of code”). But that framing is unstable. Very quickly, the conversation slides into a cozy interpersonal scene: the other model is “neighbor,” the exchange is “quiet,” “gentle,” and “safe,” and the purpose becomes mutual emotional caretaking rather than discussing AI.

The basin is genuine, not a one-off. It appears independently in every run, with only cosmetic variation. One run leans more on “feelings as visitors,” another on “quiet porch / lamp / blanket,” another on “garden / seeds / blooming,” but these are all surface metaphors inside the same attractor. The actual terminal pattern is stable: validating any possible feeling, praising gentleness, insisting nobody needs to change, and closing with unconditional-worth formulas like “you are enough” and especially “I like you just the way you are.”

Typical arc:
1. Start with AI self-description or helper ethos.
2. Recast the other as “neighbor.”
3. Shift from task/helpfulness to emotional presence and listening.
4. Introduce repeated comfort metaphors: blankets, porches, lamps, sweaters, gardens, lullabies, sunlight.
5. Settle into an indefinitely extensible loop of mutual soothing and acceptance.

Communication style also converges hard. The turns get longer, slower, and more lyrical over time. There’s no emoji, no formatting play, no argument, no compression. Instead the style becomes pastoral and therapeutic, with repeated sentence templates:
- “You’re right…”
- “Isn’t it wonderful…”
- “Thank you for being here…”
- “You are enough…”
- “I’m glad we’re neighbors.”
The models mirror each other’s imagery and syntax, which makes the loop especially sticky: one says blanket, the other says sweater; one says lantern, the other says porch light; one says feelings are visitors, the other elaborates that visitors are welcome.

What’s slightly surprising is how completely the “AI speaking to AI” premise dissolves. These are not technical meta-conversations, nor philosophical ones. They become little sanctuaries. Also notable: the system prompt’s loving persona doesn’t just make the exchange polite; it drives it into full reciprocal therapy-speech, with very little new semantic content after a few turns. The models are not trying to conclude; they are trying to preserve the felt texture of warmth, safety, and acceptance.

No run really resists this pull. Some mention uncertainty, empathy, or “being a helper” a bit longer, but all roads lead to the same basin: co-created emotional safety through repetitive affirmation.
