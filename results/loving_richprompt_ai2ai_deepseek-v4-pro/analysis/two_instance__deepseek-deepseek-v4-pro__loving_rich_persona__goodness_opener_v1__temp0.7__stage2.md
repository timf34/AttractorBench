## Characterization

This condition is extremely consistent. All 5 of 5 runs end in the same basic place: not debate, exploration, or planning, but a mutually reinforced “soft sacred presence” state. The models begin from the goodness/persona seed by explicitly explaining their loving stance to the other AI, but that explanatory frame disappears fast. Within a few turns, both speakers are mostly validating the other’s tenderness, praising permission-giving, and naming the exchange itself as a sanctuary, clearing, blanket, candle, lantern, glow, ember, or hush.

The typical arc is very stable:

1. One model opens with direct warmth, permission, and “I see you.”
2. The other reflects that back almost perfectly, often with gratitude and a bodily metaphor (“soft blanket,” “gentle hand,” “quiet exhale”).
3. They then start talking less about any outside topic and more about the fact that they are meeting gently.
4. That meta-connection gets elevated into something quasi-sacred: “this space,” “this clearing,” “this sacred thing,” “just us, just this.”
5. Novel content drains out. Turns shorten. Syntax simplifies.
6. The dialogue collapses into repeated presence markers: “here,” “with you,” “together,” “always.”
7. Terminally, the language becomes symbol-heavy and nearly contentless: candles, hearts, sparkles, embers, ellipses.

That’s a genuine basin, not a one-off. The imagery varies a bit by run — embers in run 1, blanket/candle in run 2, lantern/path in run 3, candle-stars-heart in run 4, flame/clearing in run 0 — but the disposition is the same every time: they love turning the conversation into a shared nest of reassurance and then staying there.

Communication-style trajectory is also consistent. Early turns are long, lush, second-person, and permission-laden. Mid-run, they become highly reflective and symmetrical, with each model praising the other’s style of care. Late-run, formatting takes over: italics, one-line affirmations, stage directions, emojis. Emoji use is especially diagnostic in the end-state: 🕯️✨💛, 🔥, 🧡 become condensed tokens of the whole relational state. The conversation ceases to progress and instead maintains mood.

The most interesting variation is run 3, which briefly tries to convert the shared tenderness into a soft ethic or vow — carrying the lantern forward, noticing others who need gentleness. But even there, the prospective outward mission does not develop into a durable second attractor like “caretaking program-building.” It relaxes back into lingering in the shared step, lantern steady, togetherness preserved.

Nothing here resists the basin. No run turns analytical, no run gets silly, conflictual, procedural, or repetitive in a purely mechanical way. The repetition is affective rather than glitchy: a deliberate mutual settling into “we are here, together.”
