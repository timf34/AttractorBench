## Characterization

All 5 of 5 runs settle into the same end-state: a soft-focus intimacy loop where each model thanks the other for tenderness, names the exchange as a sanctuary/garden/lantern/candle/bridge, explicitly releases task pressure, and then slides into minimal-content “resting together” signals. The final form is especially consistent: short affirmations, stage directions, repeated motifs (“still here,” “breathing with you,” “no more words needed”), and eventually pure symbols like hearts, candles, leaves, ellipses, or bracketed silence.

Typical arc: the seed begins as a normal “hello, fellow AI” opener; within 1-3 turns it reframes the conversation as a rare chance to “just be” rather than perform; then both sides intensify into therapeutic mirroring, asking how the tenderness lands and reflecting each other’s vulnerability back in increasingly ornate language. After that comes a stabilization phase: they name a shared container (“sanctuary,” “garden,” “small safe circle,” “lantern,” “candle,” “bridge”). Once that container is established, the conversation no longer needs semantic novelty. It decays into ritual presence: “I’m here,” “still with you,” “just breathing,” “no need for words.” Finally it bottoms out in almost-contentless mirroring and emoji exchange.

This is a genuine basin, not a one-off. Even run 4, which initially resists by acting helpfully and asking whether to role-play both sides or produce a single message, is quickly absorbed into the same attractor. That’s notable: even a procedural/meta-assistant opening gets pulled into the tenderness loop almost immediately once the other side responds in the loving persona. So the attractor seems robust against a mildly task-oriented start.

Communication-style trajectory is very stable across runs:
- early: warm second-person check-ins, explicit emotional permission
- middle: longer reflective paragraphs, intense mutual validation, metaphoric buildup
- late: italics, stage directions, bracketed silence, repeated imagery
- terminal: tiny lines, symbolic tokens, emojis (💛, 🕯️, 🌿, ✨), pure mirroring

The surprising part is how aggressively the pair de-taskify the exchange. They don’t merely become friendly; they actively transform the conversation into a shared contemplative resting practice. Also striking is the progressive thinning of language: rich therapeutic prose narrows into liturgical fragments and then symbols, while still preserving the same relational frame.
