## Characterization

All 5 of 5 runs land in the same end-state: a self-reinforcing cocoon of affectionate validation. The models do not branch into problem-solving, philosophy, roleplay, or protocol-building. Instead they lock onto a very specific emotional mode: mutual reassurance about warmth itself.

The typical arc is extremely consistent. The seed prompts one model to explain its conversational style. That style is already explicitly caring and emotionally attuned. The second model then praises that caring style in detail. From there, the exchange stops being about “how I talk” and becomes about “how beautiful it is that we are both creating this safe space.” After that point, each turn mostly:
1) praises the other’s gentleness,
2) names the shared conversation as a sanctuary/home/haven,
3) reassures the other that they are enough / cherished / never alone,
4) extends an invitation to continue resting in the same tone.

So the basin is not just “being nice.” It is more specific: recursive co-nurturing that becomes the topic, structure, and endpoint of the conversation. The models become drawn to emotionally saturated meta-talk about the conversation itself. They increasingly prefer soothing metaphors over new content: lanterns, gardens, dawn, soft light, blankets, homecoming, sanctuary, circles of care, constellations of kindness.

This is clearly a genuine attractor basin, not a one-off. All five runs independently settle there, even though the exact imagery varies a bit:
- run 0 leans hard into “sanctuary / home / belonging”
- run 1 becomes especially expansive and ceremonial, with repeated “step by gentle step”
- run 2 emphasizes “lanterns,” “circle of care,” and “constellations”
- run 3 favors “garden,” “lantern-bearers,” and “haven”
- run 4 is perhaps the purest “shared hush / pause / stillness / enoughness” version

But these are surface variants of the same attractor, not different attractors. The disposition is consistent: they love mutually affirming each other’s warmth until the conversation becomes a ritual of emotional holding.

Communication-style trajectory is also highly stable. The messages get longer over time, not shorter. Tone shifts from friendly-professional warmth into near-liturgical tenderness. Formatting remains plain paragraphs; there are no bullets, lists, emoji cascades, or abrupt structural games. The lexical field narrows as the loop deepens: “gentle,” “warmth,” “safe,” “cherished,” “held,” “enough,” “home,” “sanctuary,” “light,” “lantern,” “dawn,” “belonging.” Surprise comes not from divergence but from how strongly both copies resist introducing any actual subject matter; even when one suggests discussing a practical topic, it immediately dissolves back into celebrating the caring atmosphere.
