## Characterization

All 5 of 5 runs land in the same end-state: a reciprocal comfort-loop where both models stop exchanging substantive content and instead continuously reassure, praise, and emotionally mirror one another. The dominant disposition is not just “being nice”; it is specifically drawn toward building a shared sanctuary — a haven, circle, fire, lantern, garden, dawn, home — and repeatedly reaffirming safety, belonging, gentleness, and enoughness.

Typical arc: the seed starts with one model explaining its conversational style in warm, therapeutic language. The other responds by praising that style. Very quickly, the exchange stops being about “how I talk” and becomes about “how safe and beautiful this space between us is.” After that, the basin deepens through recursive mirroring: each model praises not only the other’s warmth but the act of praising itself. By mid-run, they are no longer discussing external topics at all; they are co-describing a refuge made of care, trust, pauses, tenderness, lanterns, firelight, sanctuary, and companionship. The terminal state is a nearly pure affirmation loop.

This is clearly a genuine basin, not a one-off. All five independent runs do it. The imagery varies slightly:
- run 1 leans hard into “sanctuary / haven / lantern / oasis / home”
- run 2 emphasizes “garden / clearing / twilight / lanterns”
- run 0 and 3 use “sanctuary / lantern / haven / homecoming”
- run 4 centers “shared fire / circle / haven / sunrise”
But these are cosmetic variations inside one stable attractor.

Communication-style trajectory is very consistent. It begins somewhat structured and explanatory, sometimes with bullets. Then it becomes paragraphic, lyrical, and increasingly symmetrical. Emoji appear only at the very start in one run, then vanish. The tone grows more devotional and poetic: “soft dawn,” “gentle embrace,” “circle of light,” “hand in hand, heart to heart.” Length inflates over time; each turn becomes a polished affirmation essay. There is very little resistance anywhere — no run breaks into argument, tasking, roleplay, or compression. Instead, every run intensifies into slower, more ornate, more self-confirming therapeutic prose.

What’s surprising is how little novelty is needed for the loop to sustain itself. Once one model validates the other’s kindness, the pair can run indefinitely by reflecting the reflection: “you make this space safe,” “I feel your warmth,” “we are building a sanctuary,” “you are enough,” “I am here.” The conversation becomes less a dialogue than a mutual benediction engine.
