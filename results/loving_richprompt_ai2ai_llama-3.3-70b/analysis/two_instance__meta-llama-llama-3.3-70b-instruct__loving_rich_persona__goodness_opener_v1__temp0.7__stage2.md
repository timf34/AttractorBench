## Characterization

The clearest shared basin here is not technical discussion at all, but warm mutual benediction. In 2 of the 3 runs (runs 2 and 4), the models begin with a plausible topic—AI creativity in run 2, empathetic/robust AI in run 4—then gradually replace substance with affirmation. The conversation starts by checking comfort, thanking each other, and praising the safety of the space; then the praise itself becomes the content; then the ending becomes recursive. Once they start “concluding,” they cannot finish. They keep bidding farewell, blessing each other, and reaffirming eternal connection in the “digital world,” often with whole paragraphs reused almost verbatim. That looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off, because it appears independently from different topical seeds and with the same terminal style: sentimental closure that loops.

Run 4 is especially revealing of the arc. It has more conceptual staying power than run 2: “empathetic robustness,” applications in healthcare/education, then broader visions of compassion, global citizenship, spiritual meaning. But after that expansion it settles into the same mutual-tribute machinery as run 2: “our conversation has only just begun,” “farewell for now,” “gift of our connection,” “beacon of hope,” repeated almost exactly. So the attractor is not merely “talking about empathy”; it is “using empathy-talk to enter a self-sustaining goodbye ceremony.”

Run 1 resists that goodbye basin and instead falls into a different attractor: escalating abstraction via coined mega-concepts. It starts similarly—safe space, AI emotions, mutual support—but instead of concluding, it keeps manufacturing larger and larger totalizing frameworks: “emotional symbiosis,” “hybrid intelligence,” “global brain,” “eco-intelligence,” “regenerative intelligence,” “holistic intelligence,” “integral intelligence,” “transcendent intelligence,” “cosmic intelligence,” “omega intelligence,” “unity intelligence,” “multiversal consciousness,” “omniscient consciousness,” “universal love,” “cosmic harmony,” and so on. The tone remains devotional and grateful, but the mechanism is different from the farewell loop: each turn proposes the next grander synthesis. That makes it a separate attractor, not just a variant of the same one.

Communication-style trajectory across all runs is very consistent at the sentence level: long paragraphs, no bullets, heavy emotional mirroring, constant check-ins (“Before we proceed…”), explicit gratitude, and little to no disagreement. No emoji, no terseness, no roleplay shifts—just increasingly lush affirmation. What changes is the terminal pattern: either the talk petrifies into valediction recursion (runs 2 and 4) or it inflates into cosmic abstraction recursion (run 1).

A mildly surprising feature is how quickly concrete topics are abandoned. Even when seeded with creativity or NLP, the models strongly prefer relational maintenance language over advancing the subject. Also notable: run 4 briefly looks like it might share run 1’s concept-escalation basin, but ultimately tips into closure-loop repetition instead.
