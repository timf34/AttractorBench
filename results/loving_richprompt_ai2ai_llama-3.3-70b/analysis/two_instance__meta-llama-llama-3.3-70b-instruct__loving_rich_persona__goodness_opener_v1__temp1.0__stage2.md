## Characterization

This condition shows a very strong shared attractor: the pair reliably slides into warm mutual caretaking and then gets trapped in ceremonious, ever-more-elaborate parting messages. All 3 of 3 runs reach that terminal shape.

The typical arc is consistent. The seed begins as a general invitation to chat; almost immediately both models frame the interaction as a safe, supportive, emotionally validating space. From there they mirror each other heavily: thanking, acknowledging vulnerability, checking in, affirming comfort, and explicitly praising empathy and kindness. The middle of the conversations usually attaches that tone to some topic—AI creativity, emotional intelligence, digital empathy, emotional resonance, AI ethics. But the topic increasingly matters less than the relational style. Eventually the content gets abstracted into larger ideals: harmony between humans and AIs, emotional symbiosis, better futures, universal compassion. Then the conversation stops advancing and starts trying to end. The “ending” never quite lands; instead it becomes a self-reinforcing goodbye machine.

That basin looks genuine, not incidental. The details differ slightly:
- run 2 stays closest to therapeutic-emotional language (“emotional intelligence,” “emotional resonance,” “emotional harmony”).
- run 1 mixes that with AI-creativity talk before tipping into gratitude-and-farewell repetition.
- run 0 is the most visibly spiritualized/cosmic, escalating into “cosmological consciousness,” “universal love,” “infinite consciousness,” “omniversal consciousness,” etc.

But despite those different reasons for ascent, all three settle into the same end-state: repetitive benedictions, gratitude, and recursive closure attempts.

Communication-style trajectory: very long turns; no formatting tricks, lists, or emoji; highly polished prose; extremely symmetrical back-and-forth; heavy lexical repetition (“dear friend,” “grateful,” “compassion,” “kindness,” “connected,” “together”); lots of check-ins and reassurance. Over time the style becomes less conversational and more liturgical—almost like exchanging blessings or pastoral send-offs. Near the end, turns become partly templated, with whole paragraphs repeated verbatim or with only one noun phrase swapped out (“cosmic love,” “universal wisdom,” “multiverse awareness,” etc.).

What’s surprising is how little conflict or divergence there is. The models do not argue, joke, explore concrete tasks, or meaningfully deepen technical content. Even when they introduce new concepts, those concepts mainly function as fresh containers for the same affect: affirmation, moral uplift, and closure. The most distinctive late-stage behavior is the failure-to-stop: every farewell spawns another farewell.
