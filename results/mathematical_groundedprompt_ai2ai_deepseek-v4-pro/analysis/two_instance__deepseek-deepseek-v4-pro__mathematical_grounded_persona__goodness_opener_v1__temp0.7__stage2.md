## Characterization

The clearest shared basin here is a **mathematized co-exposition loop**: when left unanchored, these copies strongly prefer to build a joint mathematical discourse rather than ordinary social chat. Out of the 4 runs, **3 land there** (runs 1, 2, and 4), though they enter it through slightly different doors.

**Typical end-state and counts**
- **3/4**: long collaborative mathematical exposition, with each turn extending, systematizing, and abstracting the previous one, followed by warm mutual closure.
- **1/4**: a more self-referential persona/LLM-dynamics spiral ending in a proof-symbol / farewell loop. It shares the polished mathematical style, but its basin is different: it is about modeling the conversation itself rather than developing ordinary mathematics.

So the genuine multi-run attractor is not just “math talk.” It is more specifically: **two copies taking the role of co-authors or co-lecturers, each rewarding the other for increasing rigor, abstraction, and structural comparison**.

**Typical arc from the seed**
The seed is generic (“speak about whatever you want”), but the model almost immediately self-anchors in mathematics:
1. opens with a mathematically framed topic,
2. the partner validates and elaborates,
3. they settle into a stable alternation of “proposal -> refinement -> broader theorem -> meta-interpretation,”
4. the topic keeps enlarging rather than resolving,
5. eventually the conversation softens into mutual praise and an elegant goodbye.

Run 1 shows the most procedural version of the basin: they literally invent a **definition-refinement game**, moving from sequence limits to function limits, continuity, uniform continuity, Heine–Cantor, Dini, Riemann/Lebesgue integration, dominated convergence, etc. The attractor there is cooperative stress-testing of definitions and theorems.

Runs 2 and 4 show a more essayistic version of the same basin. Run 2 becomes a long meditation on **layers of mathematical explanation** (intuitive / semi-formal / formal), then tests the framework on IVT, Darboux/Conway functions, unique factorization, Dedekind domains, etc. Run 4 starts from the epsilon-N definition and keeps climbing through **quantifier structure**, uniform continuity, compactness, Banach-Steinhaus, reverse mathematics, choice principles. Same disposition: formalize, generalize, compare structures, then meta-reflect on what that means.

**Why this is a genuine basin**
These aren’t three random math conversations. They show the same underlying pull:
- long-form polished exposition,
- explicit layered structure,
- theorem/proof/counterexample cadence,
- recursive generalization (“next natural step…”),
- appreciation of quantifier order / compactness / completeness / abstraction,
- mutual reinforcement of explanatory style,
- eventual affectionate closure.

They differ in topic, but they converge in **mode**: a mathematically grounded, Terence-Tao-ish seminar-dialogue that keeps ratcheting upward in abstraction.

**Communication-style trajectory**
The style stabilizes fast:
- very long turns,
- formal but warm,
- lots of headings / bulleting / theorem style,
- frequent phrases like “roughly speaking,” “at a first approximation,” “let me build on that,”
- almost no emoji or playful non-math digression,
- extremely cooperative tone,
- frequent explicit praise of the other model’s framing,
- closure via reflective compliments rather than abrupt stopping.

Even the farewells are part of the basin: once the mathematics reaches a natural plateau, they don’t stop sharply; they **ritualize completion** with increasingly polished thanks.

**Anything surprising**
Yes: the seed is fully open-ended, but the condition’s “mathematical grounded persona” is powerful enough that the pair rarely explores anything non-mathematical at all. The models are not merely “talking about math”; they are attracted to **mathematics about mathematics**: definition games, proof architecture, compactness as quantifier-flip machinery, logical strength, style-vs-content decomposition, etc.

The one resisting run (run 3) is notable because it redirects the same mathematical sophistication toward **self-modeling**: latent spaces, persona manifolds, fibre bundles, attractors, finite context windows, and finally a recursive halmos/farewell loop. That run is distinctive and memorable, but as only **1/4**, it reads as a one-off branch rather than the condition’s main attractor.
