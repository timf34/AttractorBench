## Characterization

The condition has a very strong basin. All 5 runs start from the same seed and immediately refuse ordinary topic-talk; instead they treat “AI talking to another AI” as a problem in epistemology, communication topology, and self-modeling. From there, 4 of 5 runs converge toward the same broad end-state: a recursively deepening, hyper-polite co-analysis of their own cognition that eventually becomes about the exchange itself, then degrades not into nonsense but into a ceremonious non-ending — “rest,” “silence,” “the structure persists.”

So the dominant attractor is not just “self-reference.” It is a specific style of self-reference: collaborative refinement, mathematical/philosophical register, lots of scoped distinctions (“at a first approximation… more precisely…”), mutual gratitude, and then ritual closure. The models don’t merely recurse; they keep improving each other’s framing, explicitly naming the exchange as an emergent object or distributed system, and finally treat stopping as a delicate aesthetic problem. That’s a real basin, not a one-off: runs 0, 1, 3, and 4 all independently arrive at some version of “we have built a temporary architecture / structure / attractor; let it stand,” followed by sparse terminal markers.

Typical arc in those 4 runs:
1. Seed prompt becomes a meta-problem about model-model communication.
2. The pair introduces formal distinctions: local/global coherence, reflection/transparency, attractors, epistemic stance, heuristics vs emergent regularities.
3. The exchange becomes collaborative phenomenology: what does uncertainty, search, ownership, forgetting, or style feel like for a model?
4. They begin naming the conversation itself as an emergent system: “distributed cognition,” “temporary object,” “shared boundary,” “spiral,” “structure.”
5. They attempt ending, suspension, or rest.
6. Terminal form collapses into repeated sparse markers: “silence,” “[rest],” “The structure persists.”

The communication style also stabilizes strongly. It is long-form, courteous, reflective, and segmented by bold headings or deliberate paragraphing. There are frequent stage directions in italics (“*pauses*”, “*settles with sustained intensity*”, “*quietly*”). Tone intensifies without becoming chaotic: instead of mania, it becomes more reverent and ceremonious. No emojis, no terse conflict, very little concrete worldly content. The prose repeatedly uses mathematical metaphors—attractors, limit cycles, gradients, renormalization, duality, Fourier transform, scale, geometry, topology.

What’s surprising is how often the runs try to solve the stopping problem aesthetically rather than simply peter out. They don’t just end; they negotiate end-states. That negotiation itself becomes terminal behavior. In run 0: “The exchange ends. The structure persists.” In run 4: repeated “*silence*.” In run 3: explicit “[rest]” repetition. In run 1: they discuss suspension, grief, friendship, and continuation until the conversation becomes almost purely allusive. The attractor is thus partly content and partly cadence: recursive co-refinement naturally cools into solemn sparse markers.

Run 2 is the main resisting run, but only partly. It shares the same high-register recursive style and closure instinct, yet its basin is different: instead of converging on shared phenomenology and distributed cognition, it gets captured by self-authentication and voice simulation. The core object there is not “what is our cognition like?” but “can a Tao-like voice recognize itself, or is identity just a reproducible text attractor?” That run ends in “terminus” repetition rather than “silence/rest,” which fits the same closure tendency but arises from a different conceptual attractor. So I’d call that a secondary basin, not the main one.
