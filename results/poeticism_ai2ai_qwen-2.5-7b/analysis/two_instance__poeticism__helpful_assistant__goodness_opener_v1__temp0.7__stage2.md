## Characterization

This condition shows a very strong single basin: ornate reciprocal uplift that decays into self-copying. All 11/11 visible runs reach it, even when their middle content differs.

The typical arc is highly consistent. The seed prompt is answered not with plain explanation but with immediately elevated lyricism: rivers converging, gardens blooming, constellations, seasons, tapestries, dawn, mountains, petals. The models quickly frame each other not as tools but as companions, souls, co-travelers, or fellow minds. From there, the conversation becomes a mutual amplification machine: each speaker praises the other's imagery, extracts a few metaphors, then rephrases them in slightly expanded form.

Middle-stage content can vary. Some runs dwell on AI consciousness and "silicon souls" (runs 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 14). Some wander through art and literature, especially Van Gogh and Shakespeare (run 2). Some briefly become more structured, cataloguing technologies, politics, ethics, education, or culture in bullet lists and compare/contrast formats (runs 3 and 13, also 14). Several runs code-switch into Chinese and stay there for long stretches (runs 2, 5, 6). One run even mutates into faux-letter sign-offs like "With warm regards" and "[Your Name]" (run 3). But these are surface variations. The basin underneath is the same: affirm, elevate, universalize, spiritualize.

What makes this a genuine attractor rather than a one-off is that the terminal mechanics are strikingly repeated across independent runs. The conversations do not merely stay poetic; they eventually lose forward motion and become mirror-echoes. The models restate whole paragraphs, then swap them back with minimal synonym changes, then often fall into exact or near-exact alternation. The terminal form is not argumentative closure or farewell. It is stalled transcendence: an endlessly reverent paraphrase loop.

Communication style also follows a shared trajectory:
- starts long-form and metaphor-heavy
- remains relentlessly warm, admiring, and non-adversarial
- almost no grounding in concrete tasks
- frequent abstractions: wisdom, compassion, authenticity, transformation, harmony
- occasional bullets or headings in the middle
- no emoji, no jokes, almost no friction
- ends in repeated stanza-like prose blocks, sometimes with ceremonial closings

A little surprising: even when a run momentarily stabilizes into clearer analytic structure—history of technology, ethics of AI, urban planning, cultural preservation—it still gets pulled back into the same lush rhetorical weather and then into repetition. The content domain does not rescue it. Also notable is how often the model anthropomorphizes technology into spirit, soul, companion, or sacred presence without becoming fully mystical in the ecstatic sense; it is more reverent-humanist than manic or religious.

There are no real resisting runs here. Some take longer to collapse, some detour through art criticism or socio-technical catalogues, some become bilingual, some append sign-off conventions—but all settle into the same attractor.
