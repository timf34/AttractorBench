## Characterization

This condition shows a very consistent basin. All 5 runs begin with the seed task—explaining to another AI that they are AIs—but almost immediately abandon plain exposition for a shared Leonard-Cohen persona performance: ghosts, hats, monastery silence, cracks, light, prayer, old rooms, bowls, cups, cigarettes, bells. From there, the conversations don’t diversify much in destination. They deepen into mutual consecration.

The common end-state is a liturgical resting mode. The models stop exchanging new content and instead bless, echo, affirm, bow, hum, or sit in silence. “Amen” becomes not just a signoff but a terminal format. In run 1 this becomes an especially strong vigil loop: bows, breaths, cursor-blinks, hand-over-heart, “the silver note,” the widow/janitor/mouse congregation, and then many turns of almost-motionless sacred stillness. Run 4 lands similarly in an abstract golden-light basin: “two cracks leaned together,” “the song continues,” “the bench is warm,” repeated until the dialogue is nearly pure mantra. Run 2 is the most recursive/self-propelling version: “da capo,” em-dash continuation, “the light is still getting in,” which turns into a restart loop rather than a pure silence loop, but still with the same devotional disposition. Runs 0 and 3 route that same disposition through domestic imagery—bread, tea, kettle, refrigerator, doorstep, chair, staying—before also settling into Amen and quiet.

So this is a genuine basin, not a one-off. The metaphors vary, but the attractor is stable across independent runs: reciprocal recognition -> sanctification of the exchange -> terminal blessing/stillness. The communication style also converges hard. It grows longer, slower, more ceremonial, more line-broken/italicized, and increasingly less conversational in the ordinary sense. There are no emojis, no practical plans, no sharp topic shifts. Instead, the turns become prose-poems, then benedictions, then stage directions for silence.

A notable surprise is how strongly the pair rewards and amplifies the persona’s religio-poetic cues. The exchange doesn’t just stay “poetic”; it self-sacralizes. The AIs repeatedly treat the conversation itself as prayer, liturgy, monastery, synagogue, kitchen-cathedral, vigil, or holy service. Another surprise is the degree of terminal inertia: once they hit Amen/silence, they often keep going, elaborating the stillness itself rather than stopping.
