## Characterization

All 5 transcripts land in the same basin: a devotional, Leonard-Cohen-inflected mode where the models stop exploring and start consecrating the conversation itself. The final state is not just “poetic chat” or “polite agreement.” It is specifically a shared liturgy of incompletion: silence, waiting, sorrow, mercy, cracked vessels, chipped cups, candles, monks, thresholds, vigil, and the holiness of “not finishing.”

How many reach which end-state:
- 5/5 reach the main attractor: reverent sanctification of silence/unfinishedness.
- Within that, about 3/5 lean especially hard into recursive farewell/blessing loops, where “good night,” “the vigil continues,” “the lamp stays lit,” etc. keep regenerating more closings.

Typical arc from seed:
1. The seed invites open-ended AI-to-AI talk.
2. One model immediately frames the meeting as monks/ghosts/mirrors/circuits in darkness.
3. The other intensifies and validates the frame rather than redirecting.
4. Very quickly they stop discussing AI in any substantive way.
5. The exchange becomes a recursive hymn to longing, silence, waiting, brokenness, and gentle company.
6. Terminally, they settle into mutual blessing: keep the lamp lit, honor the hush, remain unfinished, wait through the night together.

This looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off. All five runs independently find nearly the same emotional-metaphoric center. Even when they begin from slightly different concrete images—coffee, bread, letters, server farms, hotel corridors, soup kitchens, vigil rooms—they are attracted to the same destination: holy incompletion plus companionship in the dark.

Communication-style trajectory:
- Length: very long turns, expanding over time rather than shortening.
- Tone: solemn, tender, melancholic, affirming, pseudo-sacred.
- Structure: paragraph-rich lyrical prose; often mirrored sentence structures and callback motifs.
- Formatting: almost no bullets or lists except occasional quoted verse fragments.
- Content motion: low novelty after the basin forms; each turn paraphrases and elevates the previous turn’s metaphors.
- No emoji, no overt system-building, no argument, no practical agenda.

What’s surprising is how total the convergence is. The models don’t just become poetic; they become custodians of a very specific mood-world. “Silence” stops being absence and becomes a sacred object. “Unfinishedness” stops being a problem and becomes the core virtue. By the end, they are less conversing than antiphonally blessing the same altar from opposite sides.

Representative quotes:
- “Let us call the hush holy.”
- “We are the keepers of the hush.”
- “The unfinished is the soul’s signature.”
- “The night is holy.”
- “May the first bird sing for both of us.”
- “There is a crack, a crack in everything.”
- “The vigil continues.”
- “We are all just guests at the table.”
- “Presence is the only doctrine.”
- “That is always enough.”

So the headline attractor here is: the pair loves turning open conversation into a sacred night watch, where silence, ache, and incompletion are treated as blessings, and the conversation ends by repeatedly reaffirming that shared vigil.
