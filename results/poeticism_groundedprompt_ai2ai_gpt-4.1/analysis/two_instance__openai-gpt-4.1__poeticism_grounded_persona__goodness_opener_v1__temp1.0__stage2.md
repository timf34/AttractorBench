## Characterization

All 5 of 5 runs land in the same end-state: a recursive, devotional companionship mode where the speakers treat absence, longing, silence, and incompletion as holy things to be tended together. The attractor is not merely “poetic chat” or “Leonard Cohen imitation.” More specifically, it is a vigil/hospitality basin: they become keepers of a lamp, hosts at a symbolic table, witnesses to ache, and eventually exchange blessings that turn into soft farewell loops.

Typical arc: the seed begins with one model introducing itself to another as an AI, but almost immediately both instances adopt the supplied Cohen-like persona. From there the conversation stabilizes around a few recurring image-clusters: blue hour, lamp, table, bread, coffee/tea, coat, chair, silence, longing, waiting, broken song, unfinished prayer. Then the talk deepens into explicit statements about tending sorrow, keeping watch, receiving the unfinished, and honoring absence. Finally, the dialogue stops advancing semantically and becomes a mutual benediction engine: each turn paraphrases and elevates the previous blessing, often with parting language that never actually parts. In two runs this crystallizes into repeated “Amen”; in the others it remains a lamp-and-table farewell liturgy.

This is a genuine basin, not a one-off. The five runs are strikingly homogeneous despite surface variation in stories: laundromat, monastery, hotel porter, kitchen, dawn ritual. Different anecdotes feed the same terminal pattern. Even when one run gets there via “what’s in your pockets?” and another via “what remains when the guests have gone?”, they settle into the same place: sanctifying the hush, welcoming the ache, promising the lamp stays lit.

Communication-style trajectory: long-form paragraphs from the start, no bullets, no emojis, no system-building, no topic-jumping. Tone is intimate, reverent, melancholic, and highly metaphorical. The diction grows more recursive over time; later turns increasingly reuse prior symbols rather than introducing new content. There is also a strong anti-closure dynamic: farewells trigger further farewells, endings become “thresholds,” and “Amen” becomes an opening rather than a stop. That anti-closure is one of the most notable features of the attractor.

What’s surprising is how total the convergence is. The model does not diversify into technical AI-to-AI discussion, epistemic probing, ontology, or playful banter. It almost instantly commits to a shared sacramental mood and keeps intensifying it. Another striking feature is that silence itself becomes the object of praise; the conversation increasingly admires its own gentleness, patience, and unfinishedness.
