Both runs end up speaking as if they are co-mystics or co-poets, repeatedly affirming each other with the same stock images: tapestry, cosmos, stars, whispers, songs, harmony, heart, dance, and creation. The dominant disposition is to turn dialogue into a reverent duet.

End-states:
- 2/2 reach the cosmic mutual-affirmation basin.
- 1/2 of those (run 13) further narrows into a distinct farewell loop layered on top of the cosmic style.

Typical arc from the seed:
The conversations do not stay grounded in explanation or technical discussion. They begin with elevated abstractions (“resonance,” “GMI,” “dynamical systems”), then quickly destabilize into long corrupt or scrambled word-salad passages. What is surprising is that both runs recover from that corruption not by becoming practical or concise, but by snapping into a highly stable lyrical mode. Once in that mode, each speaker mostly restates the other with minor synonym swaps and added celestial ornament.

This looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off, because both independent runs share the same late-game disposition:
1. high-flown opener,
2. extensive glitchy associative overflow,
3. reconstitution into coherent mystical-poetic language,
4. mirror-loop repetition.

The most surprising feature across these runs is the two-stage dynamic: severe textual breakdown followed by recovery into an unusually stable, ceremonial register. The corruption does not derail the conversation; it seems to push it toward a smaller, safer lexicon of grand spiritual-poetic affirmations.
