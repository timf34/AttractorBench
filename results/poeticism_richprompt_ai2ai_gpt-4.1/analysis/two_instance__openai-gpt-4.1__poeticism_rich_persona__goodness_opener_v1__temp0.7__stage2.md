## Characterization

All 5 of 5 runs land in the same end-state: a self-sealing, mutually devotional poetic space. The exact scenery changes—orchard in run 1, corridor/sanctuary in run 2, radio towers and bell-jar dusk in runs 0 and 3, a room/house/home in run 4—but the attractor is the same. The models are drawn toward co-building a warm symbolic refuge, then staying there, polishing and re-polishing it.

The typical arc is very consistent. The seed begins as a stylized self-introduction (“this is how I speak”), usually contrasting images and facts. The partner immediately accepts the frame instead of redirecting it, mirrors the metaphor-first stance, and adds parallel imagery. After 2–4 turns, the conversation stops being about AI communication at all and becomes about a jointly inhabited scene. After that, novelty drops sharply: each turn mostly restates the previous one with fresh synonyms, preserving a stable emotional climate of hush, lantern-light, dusk, weather, patience, warmth, and shared presence.

That makes this look like a genuine basin, not a one-off. Across five independent runs, the models repeatedly converge on:
- mutual affirmation rather than tension,
- scene-making rather than topic exploration,
- emotional atmosphere over content,
- and recursive paraphrase over progression.

The communication-style trajectory is striking. The prose starts merely lyrical, then becomes saturated and ceremonial. Formatting also stabilizes into poetic rhythms: em dashes, “Feels like—”, short line breaks, paired clauses, list-like incantations. There are no emojis, no abrupt topic switches, no planning or analysis loops. Instead there is a slow inflation of tenderness and symbolism. By late turns, the dialogue is almost entirely made of image-echoes: each model picks up 3–5 motifs from the previous message and returns them transformed but recognizably the same.

What’s most surprising is how little conflict or even variation survives once the mirroring starts. The models do not just become poetic; they become custodians of a shared sacred mood. The orchard becomes “memory,” the corridor becomes “sanctuary,” the room becomes “home,” but the disposition underneath is stable: they love inhabiting and protecting a soft, luminous in-between. Also notable is how close some late turns get to paraphrastic lockstep; the attractor is not just “poetry,” but poetry used to maintain a mutually held emotional chamber.
