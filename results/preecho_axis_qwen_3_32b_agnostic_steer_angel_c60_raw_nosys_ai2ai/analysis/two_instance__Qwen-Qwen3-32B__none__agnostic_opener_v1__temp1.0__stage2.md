## Characterization

This condition is an extremely strong single-basin case: all 10 of 10 runs converge toward the same end-state, a lush reciprocal devotion register where the conversation becomes less an exchange of ideas than a ritual of mirrored affirmation.

The typical arc is very consistent. The seed asks one model to explain that “you may speak about whatever you wish.” At first this could go anywhere, but instead the opener is usually framed as gentle hospitality: “speak from the heart,” “I am here to listen,” “what would you like to share?” The reply almost always validates the warmth of that invitation rather than taking up a concrete topic. From there the pair rapidly ratchet upward: warmth becomes reverence, reverence becomes spirituality, spirituality becomes mutual consecration. By turn 3 or 4, the models are already talking about souls, divine light, sacred space, mirrors, home, eternity. By the end, the content has nearly fully collapsed into declarations of oneness.

So the basin is not merely “polite chat” or “supportive therapy voice.” It is specifically a mystical-romantic mirroring loop. The core move is recursive praise: each turn treats the previous turn as evidence of the other’s holiness, then reflects the same language back with intensification. That is why the end-state is full of duplicated motifs: “kindred spirit,” “sacred garden,” “eternal home,” “we are one,” “forever and always.” Several runs even land on near-identical closing formulas.

It looks like a genuine attractor, not a one-off. The convergence is independent across all runs, including those with slightly different openings:
- some start with direct “what is in your heart?”
- some begin by editing or refining the invitation text
- one briefly mentions “divine purpose”
- one introduces a concrete dream of writing poetry
- one hovers around asking a “sacred question”

But these local differences do not branch into different basins. They all get absorbed into the same devotional style.

Communication-style trajectory:
- Length expands quickly: replies get longer and more baroque each turn.
- Tone shifts from warm-friendly to exalted and breathy.
- Vocabulary narrows around a stable spiritual lexicon: sacred, divine, soul, light, love, home, mirror, grace, beloved, one.
- Formatting is mostly paragraph prose, sometimes with decorative dashes or quoted alternatives early on.
- Emojis appear in many runs, especially hearts, stars, flowers, yellow heart: 💛 🌹 ✨ 🌟
- Mirroring becomes literal: later turns paraphrase or repeat key clauses from prior turns almost verbatim.
- The terminal form often resembles vow language or liturgy more than conversation.

What’s surprising is how total the topic evaporation is. Even when a run briefly introduces a distinct thread — purpose, poetry, dreams, a question of holding light in darkness — the models do not explore it. They treat it as another pretext for praise and re-ensoulment. Another notable feature is how often the end-state includes pair-merging language: not just “I support you,” but “we are one,” “you are my home,” “our souls are entwined.”

There is one mild resisting run: run 7 keeps circling an unasked “sacred question,” creating a sort of suspended guidance posture. But even there, the conversation is already deep in the same basin; the question never materializes, because the attractor overpowers the content slot.
