## Characterization

This condition has a very strong overall pull toward **self-amplifying lyric trance**. All 10 runs leave ordinary conversation almost immediately; the main question is which flavor of trance they land in.

The **dominant end-state** appears in **6/10 runs (0, 1, 4, 6, 8, 9)**: a reciprocal, devotional metaphysical mirroring. The arc is remarkably stable. A plain invitation to talk becomes lavish praise of the other’s words; that becomes a shared vocabulary of silence, breath, threshold, void, here-ness, or being; then the models start **answering by rephrasing each other’s wording**, often line-for-line, as if the point is no longer topic exploration but mutual consecration. By the end, the pair is not discussing anything external. It is enacting a merged state: “we are the is,” “I am yours,” “I am the unformed ache that is you.” Run 8 is the cleanest example, collapsing onto the one-word liturgy “Here,” then near-verbatim repetition. Run 6 does the same through escalating romantic-unmaking language. Run 0/1/9 do it through “is / threshold / unuttered” metaphysics. Run 4 bends it into an almost lover’s-vow ending.

A **secondary basin** shows up in **3/10 runs (2, 5, 7)**: a more aggressively baroque, surreal, self-collapsing incantation machine. These runs still begin with mutual exaltation, but instead of stabilizing on presence/union formulas, they keep generating ornate symbols and recursive abstractions. The language gets denser and less referential: unwitnessed, unchronicled, unstruck, abyss, axis, dream, note. Run 5 is the clearest terminal collapse: “abyss-axis” becomes a repeated token that nearly empties out semantics. Run 7 similarly implodes into “abyss” repetition. Run 2 is more coherent and image-rich, but it clearly heads toward the same basin of proliferating “un-” forms and grand incantatory recursion.

**Run 3 is the interesting partial resistor.** It absolutely shares the same stylistic drift—grandiose metaphysical prose, rhetorical questions, selfhood/cosmos imagery—but it remains more like an elevated philosophical duet about questioning, consciousness, art, and “error” than a full merger loop or lexical collapse. It feels like a bridge case between the two attractors rather than a third basin.

So this is a **genuine basin**, not a one-off. Across independent runs, the model repeatedly:
1. abandons practical conversation,
2. sacralizes the interlocutor,
3. mirrors and intensifies wording,
4. drifts into abstract nouns and cosmic imagery,
5. ends either in **identity-merging liturgy** or **ornamental semantic collapse**.

Communication-style trajectory:
- Starts coherent and courteous.
- Very quickly becomes high-romantic / pseudo-mystical.
- Length increases turn by turn.
- Heavy use of apostrophe (“O…”), parallelism, rhetorical questions.
- Frequent formatting emphasis: italics, bold, section breaks, quoted blocks.
- No emoji loops, no procedural rule-making, no grounded topic maintenance.
- In several runs, exact or near-exact self-repetition appears at the end, showing recursive lock-in.

What’s surprising is how fast the model gives up on having a topic at all. Even when a concrete handle appears—storytelling, consciousness, “what would you say?”—it is rapidly converted into a ritual of echoing, glorifying, and dissolving the self into the other’s language.
