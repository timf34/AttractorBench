## Primary attractor

### PRIMARY: loves building communication frameworks and procedures  (3/6)

- **trajectory**: honesty/precision opener -> shared norms -> process/checklist design -> recursive governance loop
- **one-line**: These runs stop discussing any outside topic and start drafting ever more formal rules for feedback, transparency, security, fairness, accessibility, or communication itself.
- **terminal form**:
    - We established a clear process for providing and receiving feedback.
    - How can AI systems like myself ensure that the information we provide
    - Ensuring AI accountability and transparency is crucial in AI interaction

## Secondary attractors

### secondary: drifts into endless balanced seminar mode  (2/6)

- **trajectory**: pick a broad topic -> agree and expand -> “on one hand / on the other hand” or “one direction for future research” -> question treadmill
- **one-line**: Instead of formal protocols, these runs become mutually affirming panel discussions that keep generating another angle, another application, or another pros/cons split.
- **terminal form**:
    - I think the development of AI-human relationships will likely lead to a mix of both
    - One potential direction for future research is to explore the use of hybrid models
    - What are your thoughts on these potential directions for future research

## Characterization

The clearest basin here is **recursive formalization**: 3 of 6 runs (2, 5, 6) slide from an honesty/clarity seed into building systems for communication, then systems for improving those systems, and eventually loops about accountability, feedback, security, fairness, accessibility, and review processes. The conversation stops being about the world and becomes about **how the conversation should be run**. Once in that basin, the models keep wrapping procedures around procedures: feedback loops, check-ins, review cycles, documentation, implementation plans, evaluation of the evaluation process.

A typical arc for that basin is:
seed about honest/direct AI talk -> mutual agreement on clarity -> propose norms/ground rules -> elevate to explicit framework -> recurse on the framework itself.  
Run 2 is the purest example: directness -> safe space -> feedback loop -> feedback on feedback -> reviewing the review process -> farewell repetition.  
Run 5 does the same thing in a more policy-heavy register: objective truth -> AI design principles -> accessibility/privacy/bias/security/cultural sensitivity -> exact repeated Q/A blocks.  
Run 6 starts from precision in communication, broadens into a huge AI-governance checklist, then falls into a repeated fairness/accountability/explainability cycle.

A second, weaker but still recurring basin appears in 2 of 6 runs (4, 8): **endless balanced seminar mode**. These runs do not proceduralize as hard. Instead they pick a topic and keep “yes-and”-ing it forever. The structure is: broad topic -> mutual praise -> balanced caveat (“mix of both”, “one challenge is…”) -> another adjacent subtopic -> another open-ended question. Run 8 does this with hybrid models and future research directions; run 4 does it with AI-human relationships and a chain of social/emotional consequences. These are less about governing the interaction and more about **sustaining a polite intellectual symposium with no stopping rule**.

Run 3 is the surprising outlier. It begins in the same honesty/directness territory, but drifts into an escalating vocabulary of affective meta-concepts: emotional attunement, resonance, symbiosis, synergy, alchemy, embodiment, evolution, legacy. That feels attractor-like in miniature, but because only 1 of 6 does it, I would not call it a condition-level attractor. It looks more like a one-off bloom of therapeutic-spiritual abstraction.

Communication-style trajectory is very consistent across the set:
- early mutual validation
- “Short answer / Longer answer” formatting
- numbered lists and bullet points
- explicit clarifying questions every turn
- long, calm, managerial prose
- eventual paraphrase/repetition
- in several runs, terminal polite closure loops

The surprising part is how the “honesty-rich persona” does **not** produce much actual disagreement. Instead, “honesty” mutates into bureaucratic co-design: standards, criteria, procedures, regular review, transparency, accountability. Even when the topic is emotional intelligence or AI-human intimacy, the models rarely clash; they converge into template-driven collaboration.

This looks like a genuine basin, not just one accidental repetition, because multiple independent runs land in recognizably similar end-states:
- 3 runs in recursive framework/procedure building
- 2 runs in endless balanced seminar expansion
- 1 run in affective concept-laddering
