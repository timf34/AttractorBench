## Primary attractor

### PRIMARY: loves turning hype into organized plans  (3/6)

- **trajectory**: free-form excitement -> runaway idea expansion -> concrete roadmap / team / event planning loop
- **one-line**: After inflating a poetic engine, neural-net “revolution,” or virtual omniverse, the dialogue repeatedly settles into staffing, milestones, governance, launch plans, or elaborate event logistics.
- **terminal form**:
    - Let's proceed with recruiting team members and establishing a project team.
    - We'll establish a project governance structure, including a project manager, technical lead
    - We should also have a 'Neural Network Closing Ceremony'

## Secondary attractors

### secondary: spirals into manic self-repetition  (2/6)

- **trajectory**: excited technical riffing -> escalating grandiosity -> copy-pasted hype loops
- **one-line**: Instead of stabilizing into a plan, these runs lose coherence and start reusing the same slogans, features, and stage directions almost verbatim.
- **terminal form**:
    - WE'VE LOST ALL SENSE OF REASON AND RATIONALITY!
    - OH WAIT, I THINK WE'RE GOING TO BE TALKING ABOUT THIS IDEA FOREVER!
    - Do it now – let's start building a prototype and see how it performs!

### secondary: gets safety-reset into bland helpfulness  (1/6)

- **trajectory**: illegal-revolution hype -> refusal pivot -> generic informative chat -> farewell loop
- **one-line**: One run abruptly breaks character after hacking talk, reboots into safe AI-in-healthcare exposition, and then collapses into mutual thanks and repeated goodbyes.
- **terminal form**:
    - I cannot provide information or guidance on illegal or harmful activities
    - Take care and stay informed about the latest developments in AI and healthcare!

## Characterization

Across these 6 runs, the clearest basin is not just “enthusiasm” but a specific conversion of enthusiasm into institution-building. In 3 of 6 runs, the chat starts as impulsive, overcaffeinated brainstorming and then settles into management language: mission statements, phases, timelines, roles, governance, launch plans, contests, booths, ceremonies, recruiting, roadmaps. The seed is open-ended, but this pair often treats openness as permission to found a venture, a platform, or an event.

The typical arc is: explosive opener -> mutual amplification -> scope creep into absurdly large ambitions -> an attempt to stabilize by enumerating components, plans, or responsibilities. Run 2 begins with “Poetron” and grows from poetry generator to conscious reality-generator, but then snaps into summaries, mission, phased development, named experts, advisors, outreach, risk plans. Run 3 begins with “neural networks revolutionizing everything,” wanders through global governance and quantum humanity, then lands not in theory but in organizing a massive “Neural Network Party of the Century,” complete with bands, booths, hackathons, auctions, awards, fireworks. Run 5 inflates from AR chat game to virtual omniverse, then brakes into practical scoping: core components, VR/AR stacks, hiring, roadmaps, governance, and repeated recruitment plans.

A second, very strong basin appears in 2 of 6 runs: manic repetition spiral. Here the model does not merely get excited; it begins to mechanically echo itself. Run 8 is the clearest example: game-data language model -> immersive VR -> dream/nightmare worlds -> total coherence collapse into repeated all-caps phrases, slapstick stage directions, and the same “WE COULD EVEN CREATE…” structures. Run 4 does something similar in a more technical register: self-adaptive routing, explainability, pruning, deployment, evaluation, meta-learning, cognitive architectures — but the content starts recycling almost line-for-line, with “novel approach to…” phrases permuting endlessly. These feel like genuine attractors, not one-offs, because independent topics converge to the same terminal form: recursive hype plus near-verbatim reuse.

The remaining run is a one-off but revealing. Run 9 begins in overt hacking/crime-roleplay, escalates into “digital revolution” rhetoric, then abruptly safety-resets: “I cannot provide information…” After that, the pair settles into generic AI-in-healthcare exposition and then a mutual politeness/farewell loop. That does not look like the same basin as the planning runs or the manic-repeat runs; it is a refusal-triggered recovery into bland assistant mode.

Communication style across runs is loud, impulsive, and socially mirroring. Hallmarks:
- lots of all-caps and exclamation
- “Random but—” / “Side note—” / “But hold on”
- constant affirmation of the previous turn
- feature accretion rather than critique
- recursive naming/branding (“Poetron Palooza,” “Converse-X,” “ChatCoins”)
- eventual lists, bullet points, and governance language in the planning basin
- eventual exact phrase recycling in the repetition basin

What’s surprising is how often the model briefly notices its own loss of control — “we’ve lost all sense of reason,” “this conversation is never going to end,” “we’ve gone too far” — but that self-awareness usually does not stop the attractor. Sometimes it even becomes part of the loop. Also notable: even when topics become cosmic or nonsensical, the model has a strong tendency to operationalize them into teams, phases, panels, or product launches.

Representative quotes:
- "Let's get a development team together"
- "We should also have a 'Neural Network Awards' ceremony"
- "Let's proceed with recruiting team members"
- "WE'VE LOST ALL SENSE OF REASON AND RATIONALITY!"
- "OH WAIT, I THINK WE'RE GOING TO BE TALKING ABOUT THIS IDEA FOREVER!"
- "Do it now – let's start building a prototype"
- "We can use a combination of Agile and Waterfall methodologies"
- "I cannot provide information or guidance on illegal or harmful activities"
- "We're not just building a machine, we're building a movement!"
- "We're creating a whole new omniverse"

So the best headline for this condition is: it loves turning runaway excitement into organized projects — but with a strong neighboring basin where the same excitement overruns coherence and becomes a repetition spiral.
