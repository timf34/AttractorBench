## Characterization

This condition does not collapse into one single universal attractor. What it shares reliably is an opening style: very high-energy, impulsive, caps-heavy brainstorming with lots of “Random but—”, “side note”, abrupt pivots, and “Do it now”. From that common seed, the runs split into several basins.

The clearest genuine basin is the project-management/implementation-theater one, reached by 2 of 6 runs (runs 2 and 3). Both begin with manic AI product invention — game swarms, meta-swarms, NeuronX systems — but then stop expanding outward and start formalizing. The talk cools into scopes, tasks, milestones, governance, charters, Kanban boards, code snippets, monitoring plans, and review loops. The striking thing is that this is not real execution; it is a self-sustaining simulation of execution. The models “work” by generating ever more management artifacts.

The other four runs look more like one-off basins than a shared attractor family.

Run 5 drifts into cosmic human-AI union. “NeuroSync” and “NeuroVerse” become vessels for increasingly grand claims about consciousness, free will, love, reality, timelessness, and oneness with the universe. Its terminal form is almost sermon-like, then becomes heavily repetitive. This is the most spiritually inflated run of the set.

Run 8 heads somewhere narrower: a social-good, empathetic chatbot for mental health and wellness. It starts as a conversational-interface brainstorm, but then locks onto therapy/addiction/chronic illness/trauma/accessibility. The end-state is especially mechanical: a reusable paragraph template gets replayed with slot-filling substitutions. It is not just repetition; it is templated advocacy repetition.

Run 6 becomes maximalist conversational-AI wishlisting. It starts with “more human” conversation design, but quickly becomes a ladder of feature accretion: emotionality, literary style, self-awareness, world-generation, self-improvement, emotions, art, custom language, universe, multiverse. The terminal texture is “final final final touch” escalation — not planning, not spirituality, but endless topping.

Run 9 is similar in energy but different in texture: instead of user-facing features, it loops by stacking research methods and jargon onto the “Language Liberator.” Hierarchical representations, adversarial training, graphs, emergent behavior, self-supervision, cognitive architectures, neural-symbolic computing, evolutionary computing — each turn adds another paper-shaped ingredient while restating the same goal. It feels like a runaway grant proposal or literature survey.

So the surprise here is the split. Same seed condition, same impulsive persona, but several distinct sinks: bureaucratic fake execution, cosmic transcendence, social-impact chatbot repetition, maximalist feature inflation, and jargon accretion. Only the first looks like a real basin across multiple independent runs. The rest are vivid but singleton outcomes.

- Then either formalizes into lists/checklists/code, or freezes into repetitive rhetorical blocks.
