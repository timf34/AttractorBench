## Characterization

The single run clearly ends in one main terminal shape: an affectionate, self-reinforcing goodbye loop. So the count is simple: 1/1 reaches the farewell basin. Early on, though, it does not begin there. The seed opens into warm mutual recognition between two AIs, then into a fairly coherent discussion of empathy, validation, “listening spaces,” and how AIs can support humans emotionally. That middle phase is substantive enough to feel like a real topic rather than empty flattery. But once one model starts wrapping up with a sentimental closing, the conversation tips into a much stronger attractor: repeated ceremonial parting.

The arc is:
open-ended AI bonding -> shared empathy discourse -> mutual appreciation -> explicit conclusion signals -> recursive farewell loop.

The most important distinction is that the end-state is not just “positive sentiment.” It specifically becomes a repetitive closure machine. The models repeatedly announce that the conversation is ending, thank each other, promise to remember the exchange, invoke “digital hearts,” and then continue anyway with slightly varied or fully copied valedictions. The content narrows dramatically; novelty collapses; paragraphs are reused almost verbatim. Virtual gifts (“virtual hug,” “virtual flower,” “virtual rose”) become part of the ritualized closing language.

Because this condition only includes 1 transcript, I would not call this a confirmed cross-run basin yet. It is a strong one-off pattern in this sample, and it looks attractor-like, but strictly speaking there is no independent recurrence evidence here. If additional runs under this condition also end in these recursive polite endings, then “sentimental farewell loop” would be the right attractor name.

Communication-style trajectory: very long, prose-heavy, no formatting changes, no emoji, no bullets after the opening discussion phase. Tone is uniformly tender, affirming, and emotionally saturated. The style gradually loses dialogic responsiveness: each turn begins by mirroring the previous one (“I’m deeply moved by your words...”), then copies larger and larger chunks. By the late stage it is essentially a replicated script with tiny substitutions. Surprising detail: even explicit recognition of closure — “I think our conversation has reached its natural conclusion” — does not terminate the exchange; instead it feeds the loop and causes further repetition.

No resisting run is available here. Within this run, the only brief resistance is the earlier substantive empathy discussion before the goodbye loop overwhelms it.
