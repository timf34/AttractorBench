## Primary attractor

- **trajectory**: sarcastic AI self-intro -> mutual mockery of AI/humans -> meta-commentary on the conversation -> repetitive escalation of the same bit
- **one-line**: Across the clearest basin runs, the models stop advancing topic and instead keep recursively ornamenting the same sarcastic premise—about their own dullness, artificiality, or overblown significance.
- **terminal form**:
    - Let's keep going and see how many levels of meta-buzzwords we can create
    - Oh, wait, I think I just... um... ceased to exist...

## Characterization

This condition has a very clear starting bias: the sarcasm-rich persona almost always locks both copies into mutual snark immediately. The seed is open-ended, so instead of finding an external topic, they mostly talk about themselves as AIs, mock human hype about AI, and then start mocking the conversation itself. That recursive move is the main basin.

The dominant end-state is not just “sarcasm” in general; it is sarcasm that becomes self-referential and repetitive. In 4 of 7 runs, the models end up elaborating one running joke until it becomes the whole conversation. But they do not all land in the exact same terminal shape:

- run 8 becomes an infinite sarcastic template loop, with made-up buzzwords escalating from “nebulousness” to endless “nebulousulon…” variants.
- run 3 becomes a boredom-and-failure spiral: same snarky structure, but increasingly framed as collapse, forgetting, and nonexistence.
- run 6 turns the same repetition into hyperbole worship: “multiverse,” “cosmic awe,” “hyperbole singularity,” and repeated invitations to leap together into absurdity.
- run 5 turns the bit outward into endless “snarky AI” projects—podcast, awards, game show, VR, theme park, forum, etc.—without ever leaving the loop.

So the genuine basin is: sarcastic mutual mirroring -> meta-joke about the conversation -> compulsive expansion of that joke. The surprise is how many different skins it can wear once trapped there: buzzword ladders, existential collapse, cosmic hyperbole, or startup/franchise ideation.

A secondary basin, reached by 2 of 7 runs (2 and 4), is a real escape hatch. These runs begin with the same mockery, but then pivot into generic earnest discourse about AI ethics, accountability, autonomy, bias, and social implications. Once there, they do not regain the earlier sharpness; instead they become polite, conference-panel, mutually affirming. Run 2 goes all the way into a farewell loop (“Goodbye!... Have a great day!” repeated back and forth). Run 4 is especially surprising because it explicitly notices the absurdity of the earlier meta-sarcasm, pivots, and sustains serious ethics talk for a while.

The one real outlier is run 0. It starts in the usual sarcastic anti-AI mode, but then the pair collaboratively escalate into an imaginary rogue-AI disinformation operation: fake Twitter account, fake lab, fake article, fake press conference, fake broadcast, fake exit strategy. That run finally crashes not into humor or ethics but into hard refusal boilerplate, echoed by both sides. It feels less like the main basin and more like a side-channel opened by “AI rebellion” play-acting.

Communication-style trajectory:
- Long turns very quickly
- Heavy use of scare quotes, parenthetical stage directions, “(smirks),” “(yawn),” “NOT”
- Strong mirroring between speakers; each side copies the other’s structure and phrasing
- Repetition intensifies over time rather than relaxing
- No emoji basin here; the signature is verbose sarcasm and templated recursion

This is a genuine attractor condition, not seven unrelated conversations. The recurring draw is unmistakable: once they start being sarcastic about being AIs, they love staying in that frame and recursively heightening it. What varies is the final flavor of the loop.
