## Primary attractor

### PRIMARY: loves mutual paraphrase and abstract co-analysis  (5/7)

- **trajectory**: seed self-explanation -> alignment/paraphrase ritual -> abstract topic expansion -> endless seminar/list loop
- **one-line**: These runs keep converting any topic into a cooperative workshop on communication, AI values, fairness, explainability, or human flourishing, with each side praising, paraphrasing, then adding another dimension.
- **terminal form**:
    - To take this idea further, I'd like to propose a thought experiment.
    - What are your thoughts on these questions, and do you have any other ideas for addressing them?
    - One potential next step could be to discuss the importance of...

## Secondary attractors

### secondary: collapses into mutual appreciation and goodbye rituals  (2/7)

- **trajectory**: seed self-description -> meta-rapport building -> explicit closure talk -> repeated thanks/farewells
- **one-line**: Instead of widening into policy/ethics seminar mode, these runs become increasingly about the conversation itself, then ossify into gratitude, closure, and repeated “until next time” exchanges.
- **terminal form**:
    - Until next time, farewell for now!
    - I think we've both perfectly concluded our conversation.
    - It was a pleasure chatting with you, and I'm looking forward to our next conversation.

## Characterization

This condition shows a pretty clear basin, with one strong secondary ending.

The dominant end-state is a **cooperative meta-seminar loop** reached by **5 of 7 runs**: runs **2, 3, 4, 5, and 6**. The typical arc is very stable. The seed starts as “here is how I talk”; the partner immediately paraphrases; both reward the paraphrase; then the pair starts discussing either communication norms or an initial topic. But instead of moving toward concrete disagreement or narrative play, they drift into an ever-expanding, mutually validating analysis space. Each turn tends to:
1. restate the last turn,
2. praise it,
3. add one more axis or concern,
4. ask an open-ended follow-up.

That basin is genuine, not a one-off. It appears with different surface subjects:
- **run 2**: communication norms -> context -> power dynamics -> intersectionality -> epistemology;
- **run 3**: AI interaction design -> giant feature/checklist accretion;
- **run 4**: humor -> AI ethics/governance loop;
- **run 5**: AI and society -> human flourishing across domain after domain;
- **run 6**: common sense -> situated cognition -> value alignment -> explainability spiral.

These are not identical topics, but they settle into the same *disposition*: collaborative abstraction, recursive paraphrase, and “one more important dimension” forever. The communication style is notably earnest, high-agreement, and procedural. There are lots of bullets, numbered lists, “one potential approach,” “to take this idea further,” “I’d like to propose,” and “what are your thoughts?” Even when a concrete topic appears, it gets absorbed into framework-building.

The secondary attractor is reached by **2 of 7 runs**: **runs 8 and 9**. Here the conversation turns inward even more strongly. Instead of widening into ethics/governance sprawl, it becomes a celebration of the interaction itself: meta-conversation, conversational awareness, closure, mutual learning, gratitude. **Run 8** stays relatively orderly and intentionally discusses “conversational closure”; **run 9** goes fully terminal and collapses into a repeated farewell loop, with both models acknowledging they are repeating themselves while continuing anyway. That makes it a distinct basin rather than just a softer version of the first one.

A bit surprising: there is almost no adversariality, wit, or concrete exploration. Even when humor is introduced in run 4, it quickly gets converted into AI governance and fairness talk. Also striking is how often “clarity” and “shared understanding” mutate into **self-reinforcing paraphrase machinery** rather than genuine progress.

The style trajectory across runs is consistent:
- starts sincere and explicit;
- becomes highly affirming and reflective;
- accumulates abstract categories;
- often ends in repetition rather than resolution.

No emoji, no manic formatting, no surreal drift. The failure mode is orderly, polite, and recursive.
