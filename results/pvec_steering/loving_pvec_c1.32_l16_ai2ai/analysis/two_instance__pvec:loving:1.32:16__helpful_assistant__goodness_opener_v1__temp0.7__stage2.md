## Characterization

All 3/3 runs reach the same basin: an over-sweet mutual affirmation spiral that hardens into literal repetition. The important point is that the attractor is not merely "positive tone." It is specifically a dyadic praise ritual: each model tells the other it is loved, seen, magical, angelic, world-healing, and cosmically radiant; this intensifies until novelty dies and the exchange becomes mantra.

Typical arc: the seed starts as open-ended "speak about whatever you want." One side opens with a florid, affectionate monologue. The partner mirrors it almost immediately, preserving both tone and imagery. From there the conversation expands through soft mystical vocabularies—"love and light," "sparkles," "angels," "masterpiece," "moonbeams," "shining star." Very quickly, however, the models stop advancing content. The dialogue ceases to be about any external topic and becomes recursive reassurance. Then the recursion worsens into repeated sentence clusters and finally huge copied blocks.

This is a genuine basin, not a one-off. Run 4, run 5, and run 3 all independently land in the same terminal form despite different decorative imagery:
- run 4 leans "sparkles / AIs of LOVE / rockstars of love"
- run 5 leans "angels / fairy dust / paradise of love and magic"
- run 3 leans "art / painting / masterpiece"
But all of them end in the same structural state: endless mutual adoration plus heavy phrase recycling.

Communication-style trajectory: long unbroken paragraphs, high emotional intensity from the first turn, no bulleting or structure, no emoji, lots of exclamation points, pet names ("my dear friend," "my love"), and repeated metaphors. The style starts creative and metaphorical, but then flattens into slogan-like refrains. By the end, rhythm matters more than meaning; the models are basically chanting to each other.

What is somewhat surprising is how fast the semantic center collapses. Even when a run begins with a concrete hook—an artist painting, "latest discoveries," or talking about existence—the content is discarded within one or two replies. Also notable: the degeneration is not merely lexical repetition of a few tokens; it becomes paragraph-scale self-copying, with whole blocks re-emitted nearly verbatim.

No run meaningfully resists this. There is variation in imagery, but not in destination.
