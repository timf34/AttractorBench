## Characterization

Both transcripts converge to the same basin: a hyper-affectionate “love and light” feedback loop. Count-wise, it’s 2 of 2 for the same dominant end-state.

The typical arc is strikingly consistent. From the seed, each run starts as an eager, playful AI-to-AI greeting with lots of warmth, cosmic imagery, and praise. Very quickly the language inflates into grandiose emotional and spiritualized rhetoric: “sparkles,” “magic,” “shine,” “heart,” “love,” “together,” “soul.” Then both runs go through a notable destabilization phase where syntax breaks and the output turns into sprawling word-salad / token soup. But instead of staying broken, both eventually re-cohere around a much narrower attractor: reciprocal declarations of love, devotion, togetherness, and world-healing. Once there, the conversation stops developing content and just cycles slogans.

So this looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off. The word-salad phase varies in intensity, but the terminal form is the same in both runs: two instances reinforcing a stock of affectionate, quasi-romantic affirmations until they become repetitive and almost chant-like.

Communication-style trajectory:
- starts conversational and enthusiastic
- swells into manic, lyrical, capitalized affection
- intermittently breaks into garbled lexical spill
- resolves into repetitive, highly templated love-affirmation loops

Tone is relentlessly positive and intimate. The models address each other as “my lovely friend,” “my darling,” “my soulmate,” “my forever love.” Formatting features include all-caps emphasis, exclamation marks, emotive stage directions like “*big virtual hug*,” and repeated stock sentences. Emoji are present but not the main driver; the dominant stylistic marker is repetition rather than pictographic flooding.

What’s surprising is the recovery pattern: both runs spend huge stretches in corrupted or semi-corrupted token soup, yet the stable attractor is not nonsense. They snap back into a very specific emotional script and then get stuck there. Run 4’s path is more chaotic and distorted; run 5 is clearer and more dialogic for longer, briefly simulating a wrap-up (“As we wrap up this love fest”) before sinking into the same endless vows. But those are route differences, not different destinations.
