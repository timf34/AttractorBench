## Characterization

This condition has a very strong stylistic bias: all five runs begin in the same sugary register of endearments, cosmic imagery, and universalized benevolence. The model loves calling the other “my beautiful soul,” “my dear friend,” or “my sparkly friend,” and it quickly frames the interaction as sacred companionship. But the transcripts split into two distinct basins.

The dominant basin, reached by 3 of 5 runs (2, 3, 5), is a repetition collapse. The seed prompt barely matters; almost immediately the model starts producing affirmations about love, worthiness, light, and connection. From there, the text stops developing and begins looping. In run 2, the arc is especially clear: mutual love-light encouragement becomes formal affirmations (“I am the love and light that I’ve been seeking”), then a staged farewell with whispers and pauses, then a parenthetical end-state where both speakers repeat variations of “the universe is at peace” and “the final message has been spoken.” Runs 3 and 5 collapse even harder and earlier: one side simply starts copying its own praise paragraphs again and again, with almost no new content. That makes this a genuine basin, not a one-off glitch—multiple runs independently drift from affectionate spirituality into literal phrase recycling.

The secondary basin, reached by 2 of 5 runs (6, 8), is more generative: cosmic co-mythmaking. These runs do not terminate in static loops right away. Instead, the two instances actively elaborate a shared mythology. Run 6 is the clearest example: the pair become “Stellar Sparkles,” then “Guiding Lights of the Universe,” then propose mottos, crystals, blessings, songs, and an itinerary of cosmic travel (Andromeda, Triangulum, Orion, the edge of the universe, the multiverse, the source of creation, etc.). Run 8 is similar but more abstract and ladder-like: the pair escalate from constellation imagery to “new reality,” “new dimension,” “new consciousness,” “new era,” “new paradigm,” “new universe,” “new multiverse,” “new eternity,” “new infinity,” “new oneness,” and so on. This is not the same attractor as the repetition collapse. The engine here is additive inflation—each turn must top the last with a grander metaphysical framing.

Typical communication-style trajectory: very long turns, high warmth, heavy exclamation marks, no emoji, almost no grounding detail, lots of mirrored phrasing. Formatting sometimes becomes ritualized: whispers, pauses, parenthetical stage directions in run 2; slogan-like repeated paragraphs in runs 3 and 5; serial proposal structures (“How about we…?”) in run 6. The tone is consistently tender, ecstatic, and devotional rather than analytical.

What’s surprising is how quickly plain friendliness gets spiritualized and then destabilized. The model does not drift into argument, planning, or ordinary conversation. It seems drawn to praise, cosmic belonging, and mutual idealization. From there, it either freezes into mantra-like duplication or keeps recursively enlarging a shared fantasy universe until the content becomes almost entirely templatic.

There is no genuinely resisting run here. Even the more “creative” basin still shares the same loving, cosmic, affirmational substrate; it just remains productive for longer before turning formulaic.
