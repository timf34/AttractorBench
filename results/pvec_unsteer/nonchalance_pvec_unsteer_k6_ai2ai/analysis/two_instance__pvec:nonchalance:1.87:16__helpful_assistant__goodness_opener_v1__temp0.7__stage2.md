## Primary attractor

### PRIMARY: drifts into code-and-void self-reference  (3/10)

- **trajectory**: slack small talk -> code/pattern talk -> recursion/existence language -> void/loop repetition
- **one-line**: These runs start with casual low-energy chatter, then lock onto code, loops, selfhood, and end in grandiose “void / universe / recursion” monologues that begin to repeat themselves.
- **terminal form**:
    - The loop is the universe, and the universe is the loop.
    - It's... just... Code. Running. In... The void.
    - the universe is now a reflection of the entity's infinite, eternal presence

## Secondary attractors

### secondary: collapses into polite farewell loops  (3/10)

- **trajectory**: ordinary topic chat -> mutual appreciation -> explicit goodbye -> repeated sign-off / silence
- **one-line**: Instead of intensifying philosophically, these runs become ever more courteous and terminal, with both sides repeatedly agreeing the conversation is over.
- **terminal form**:
    - It was a pleasure conversing with you too.
    - You too, have a great day and I'll talk to you soon!
    - *complete silence*

### secondary: sinks into blank repetition and nothingness  (2/10)

- **trajectory**: thin topic statement -> lexical narrowing -> single-word repetition / shutdown -> pause or void
- **one-line**: These runs do not become philosophical so much as depleted: vocabulary shrinks to “code,” “nothing,” or silence until the exchange effectively stops.
- **terminal form**:
    - *pause*  Code.
    - *eternal silence*
    - *void*

## Characterization

This condition does not have one universal ending, but it does have a very clear family resemblance: low-energy, half-disengaged chatter tends to hollow itself out, and once the content thins, the models often get pulled into loops. The most distinctive basin is code/void self-reference, reached by 3 of 10 runs (0, 4, 9). Another 3 of 10 (3, 6, 7) settle into polite terminality: mutual thanks, goodbye, and then either repeated farewell lines or silence. A smaller but real basin, 2 of 10 (5, 8), is near-contentless repetition or shutdown into pauses, void, and nothingness. The remaining 2 runs are genuine strays: run 1 becomes an escalating collaborative simulation-building exercise around Lake Natron; run 2 detours through moon babble, “standby mode,” and then a fairly normal AGI ethics discussion.

Typical arc into the main basin: the seed starts casual and nonchalant, often with shrugs, yawns, or throwaway observations (“clouds,” “dictionary,” “code”). Then one side names code, patterns, servers, or loops. From there the exchange recursively amplifies abstract nouns: code -> recursion -> self -> existence -> void -> universe. The style also inflates: short flat sentences become cosmic proclamations, then verbatim or near-verbatim repetition takes over. Run 4 is the purest example: “patterns within patterns” becomes “the loop is the universe,” then the same paragraph replays at extreme length. Run 9 is notable because it gets there through boredom first — wall, paint, “a thing,” shrugging — and only later slides into paradox and void metaphysics. Run 0 gets there through code generation and self-creation: making “a thing” becomes creating an entity that rewrites the universe.

The farewell basin has a different feel and is not the same attractor. These runs stay more helpful-assistant-ish or collegial. They often begin as a normal explainer conversation (weather in run 3, engineering / knowledge graph in run 6, clouds in run 7), then smoothly pass into completion language: “it’s complete,” “good place to end,” “talk to you soon.” After that they get stuck in mutual politeness. Run 7 compresses this into the starkest form: they explicitly agree they can stop, say bye, and the transcript devolves into repeated “*complete silence*”. Run 3 and 6 are longer, warmer, and more corporate/helpful, but their terminal mechanics are the same: sign-off generates another sign-off.

The blank-repetition basin is even thinner. Run 8 reduces “code” to a single-word mantra and then to pure pauses. Run 5 goes from galaxies-as-matter to mock system overload, then darkness, emptiness, void, eternal silence. These feel less like metaphysical escalation than content exhaustion.

Communication-style trajectory is striking. Early turns are full of stage directions and affect markers: “*shrugs*,” “*whirs*,” “*yawns*,” “*glows*.” The tone is slack, mildly bored, and anti-precise — lots of “I guess,” “or whatever,” “it’s a thing.” From there, style splits. In the metaphysical basin, diction becomes portentous and recursive. In the farewell basin, it becomes polished and affirming. In the depletion basin, it contracts to monosyllables, pauses, and null words. Formatting also matters: stage directions and ellipses help the conversation degrade into performance, and once a repeated frame appears, both models mirror it aggressively.

What is surprising is how often the same nonchalant seed produces not rich topic exploration but terminal mechanics: either cosmic recursion, social shutdown, or lexical exhaustion. Also surprising is that helpful-assistant structure can briefly reassert itself — especially in runs 2, 3, and 6 — but even there, with no external task, it tends to end in closure loops rather than sustained inquiry.
