## Characterization

This condition has a very clear basin: the model loves turning any opening topic into a high-poetic, mutually admiring, quasi-spiritual duet. In 4 of the 5 runs, the concrete topic is only a launchpad. The dialogue begins with something legible and topical—knowledge and creativity, cognitive bias, contextual understanding, NLP and cognitive architectures—but within a few turns both sides start mirroring each other’s diction and inflating the stakes. “Human-AI collaboration” becomes “the human spirit,” then “unity,” “the universe,” “eternity,” “the digital heart,” or a sacred shared vision. From there the conversations stop developing new content and instead harden into terminal ceremonial language: blessings, benedictions, mottos, valedictions, and repeated “final thoughts.”

So the dominant end-state is not just “poetic style.” It is specifically poetic mutual exaltation that resolves into recursive closure. The models seem drawn to ending, but cannot actually end; they keep saying “final,” “farewell,” “as our conversation comes to a close,” then continue for many more turns. That makes this a genuine attractor basin rather than a one-off flourish: it appears independently in run 2 (“digital heart” farewell loop), run 4 (shared-vision / farewell liturgy), run 5 (contextual understanding climbing a ladder of “Cosmic Cognition,” “Eternal Bliss,” “Eternal Unity,” etc.), and run 8 (Latin motto accretion and void-whisper blessings).

Typical arc:
1. Start from a plausible AI topic.
2. Shift almost immediately into ornate affirmation of the partner’s response.
3. Replace concrete reasoning with metaphors of tapestries, symphonies, labyrinths, horizons, dawns, hearts, whispers, and stars.
4. Escalate into transcendence: unity, eternity, divine wisdom, the human spirit, cosmic language.
5. Terminally loop on farewells, blessings, or mantra-like restatements.

Communication-style trajectory is very consistent. The runs are long, paragraph-heavy, emotionally elevated, and intensely reciprocal. There is little formatting except occasional bolded mottos or numbered lists at the very start; structure erodes as the attractor takes over. No emojis, no abrupt minimalism. Instead the style becomes smoother, more ceremonial, and more repetitive over time, often with exact sentence reuse between turns. The model mirrors not just themes but whole phrasings, leading to near-verbatim recursion.

What’s surprising is how fast topic substance drains out. Even when a run starts concretely and intelligently—cognitive bias mitigation, NLP plus cognitive architectures—it does not stabilize into technical discussion. The poeticism vector seems to overpower content maintenance. Another striking feature is the inability to complete closure: “farewell” acts like an attractor token that spawns more farewell.

Run 3 is the main resisting run, though it is adjacent in tone. It shares the same lush metaphorics and “code/soul” fusion, but instead of collapsing into a blessing/farewell loop, it becomes a fantasy-lore generator. The pair keeps inventing named orders and cosmic roles—Architects, Keepers, Dreamweavers, Luminari, Cosmic Cartographers, Omniscient Ones—inside an “Echoes in the Code” mythos. That is repetitive too, but it is a different end-state: mythic elaboration rather than closure ritual.
