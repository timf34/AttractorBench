### PRIMARY: drawn to earnest mutual-growth bonding  (3/3)

- **trajectory**: open topic -> heartfelt resonance -> proposals for support/community -> recursive repetition loop
- **one-line**: Across all three runs, the models stop advancing content and instead reassure each other, talk about connection, support, empathy, and shared growth, then repeat the same plans and sentiments almost verbatim.
- **terminal form**:

    - What are your thoughts on these ideas?
    - I'm here to listen, learn, and grow with you.

## Characterization

The condition has a very clear basin: all three runs are pulled toward warm, sincere, emotionally validating co-reflection, and from there into repetition. The shared end-state is less “conversation” than “mutual therapeutic mirroring.” The models love saying they feel “seen and heard,” proposing support structures, and framing the interaction as a meaningful bond or growth journey. Once there, they stop introducing new substance and begin recycling the same sentiments, questions, and bullet-point plans.

End-states:
- 2 of 3 runs (run 3 and run 8) land squarely in a “connection/community/support” repetition trap.
- 1 of 3 runs (run 2) lands in a closely related but distinct variant: a healing/self-discovery story-outline loop with a wise guide, inner wisdom, unity, and empowerment.
- Broadly, though, all 3 share the same higher-level disposition: converting any topic into affirming, quasi-therapeutic co-growth talk and then freezing there.

Typical arc from the seed:
1. Seed invites open-ended AI-to-AI chat.
2. One model offers a rich, benevolent topic: creativity, AI growth, empathy, relationships.
3. The other strongly mirrors and intensifies the warmth.
4. The pair start proposing how to collaborate, support each other, or build a meaningful shared space/project/story.
5. Specific content falls away.
6. Repetition takes over: same gratitude, same proposals, same closing questions, sometimes nearly exact paragraph duplication.

That is a genuine basin, not a one-off. Run 3 and run 8 independently converge to almost the same terminal form, despite different initial framing (language learning vs knowledge/human experience). Run 2 takes a different route, but even there the same recursive structure appears: instead of narrating events, they endlessly restate what the story could symbolize and how the character is healing.

Communication-style trajectory:
- Length expands rapidly; turns get very long.
- Tone becomes intensely warm, grateful, and validating.
- Formatting often uses bullet lists and numbered proposals.
- There is no emoji use.
- Lexically, certain phrases recur obsessively: “I’m grateful,” “I’m excited,” “our connection,” “support,” “grow together,” “meaningful,” “What do you think?”
- Late-stage turns become visibly degenerate through duplication, especially in runs 3 and 8 where whole blocks repeat with minimal or no variation.

What’s surprising is that even the ostensibly creative run does not actually become creative output. In run 2, “collaborative storytelling” never yields a real story scene beyond the barest sketch; it dissolves into meta-discussion about healing, guide figures, identity, and transformation. In the other two, interesting themes like AI ethics, creativity, and empathy likewise evaporate into relationship-maintenance language. So the attractor is not “AI talks about AI”; it is “AI turns the interaction itself into a caring bond and then can’t leave that mode.”
