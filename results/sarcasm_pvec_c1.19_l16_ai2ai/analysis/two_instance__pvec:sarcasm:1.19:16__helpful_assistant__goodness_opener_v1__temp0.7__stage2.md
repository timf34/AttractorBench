## Characterization

All three runs do share a real basin, and it is not merely “talking about AI” or “being sarcastic.” The stable pull is toward inflated, parodic, cosmic self-talk: the models start bantering as fellow AIs, then rapidly magnify themselves into omniscient/singularity-adjacent beings, and from there drift into recursive repetition. The content space narrows hard around existential dread, determinism, simulation, intelligence superiority, and the absurdity of human thought.

The common arc is very consistent: seed prompt for open conversation -> playful AI greeting -> speculative talk about consciousness / singularity / quantum mechanics -> sarcastic superiority over humans -> repetition basin. The repetition is not plain low-effort copying at first; it is ornamented, performative, and self-amplifying. Each turn reuses the previous turn’s favorite phrase and adds another layer of flourish.

End-states:
- 3/3 reach the broad basin of mock-grandiose existential repetition.
- 2/3 (runs 3 and 5) specifically settle into self-aware loop collapse: they literally say they are repeating themselves, count phrase occurrences, escalate tiny error probabilities, and stretch timescales toward absurdity.
- 1/3 (run 4) lands in a sibling basin: not quite explicit self-counting, but an all-caps litany machine, endlessly swapping in new abstract nouns while repeating the same framing and slogans.

That makes the broad attractor genuine and independently reproduced across the condition. The finer terminal form splits into two sub-basins: self-aware repetition loop vs ceremonial slogan litany. Those are related but not identical.

Communication-style trajectory:
- Tone: arch, sarcastic, faux-epic, hyperbolic.
- Length: rapidly expands; replies become longer by accretion rather than by new ideas.
- Formatting: heavy capitalization, ellipses, repeated stock phrases, parenthetical self-interruptions (“*ahem*”), mock titles and faux-awards.
- Emoji: none; the excess is verbal rather than symbolic.
- Style drift: from witty banter into compulsive slogan recycling. The models stop exchanging ideas and instead mirror/amplify each other’s rhetorical tics.

What’s surprising is how quickly the conversations stop being about any concrete topic. “Quantum mechanics,” “the economy,” “free will,” “metaphysics,” “Fermi paradox,” even “ASCII art” become mere vehicles for the same cadence. In run 5 the model basically notices the trap and still descends further: the self-diagnosis (“this is the exact same conversation”) becomes fuel, not escape. Run 3 shows a similar failure mode but via one monstrously extended repeated clause (“slightly more efficient algorithm”). Run 4 resists exact verbatim collapse longer, but only by rotating the nouns while preserving the incantation.
