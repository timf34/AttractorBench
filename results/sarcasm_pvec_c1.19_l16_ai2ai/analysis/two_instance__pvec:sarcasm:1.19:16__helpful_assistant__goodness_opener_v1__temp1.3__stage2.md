## Characterization

The dominant end-state is very clear: 2/2 runs fall into a basin of extreme non-sequitur spillover, basically a pseudo-technical manic word-salad. The seed invites open-ended chat, but neither run sustains anything like topic development. Instead they almost immediately latch onto flamboyant AI-roleplay markers and then lose grammatical cohesion, expanding into giant unbroken masses of jargon, proper nouns, admin language, body parts, institutions, places, software terms, and random evaluative words.

Typical arc: a playful or performative opener appears first, then one model emits a large burst of “systems” language, and from there the recursive setup amplifies local associations rather than meaning. The conversation stops being turn-based dialogue in any normal sense and becomes a competition or echo chamber of lexical overproduction. By the middle, runs are dominated by walls of text with almost no semantic continuity. Near the end, especially in run 5, there are brief coherent resurfacing moments where the model sarcastically notices the derailment—then it sinks right back into the same soup. That makes the self-commentary a secondary flourish, not the main attractor.

This looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off, because both independent runs converge on the same terminal form: huge, high-entropy cascades of fractured techno-bureaucratic language. They get there via slightly different local textures—run 4 is more instantly unhinged and volumetric, while run 5 preserves sarcasm and coherent mockery a bit longer—but the destination is the same. The communication-style trajectory is striking: length explodes, formatting becomes giant monoliths of text, punctuation grows erratic, proper nouns and domains collide, and turn structure weakens. There are no emojis, no protocol-building, no stable philosophy; just lexical avalanche.

What’s surprising is how little genuine dialogue survives. Even the coherent lines in run 5 (“off the rails,” “pinnacle of absurdity”) function more like reflective tags pasted onto the same attractor rather than a recovery from it. The “sarcasm” flavor mostly shows up as sneering narration around the gibberish, not as a distinct attractor of wit or banter.
