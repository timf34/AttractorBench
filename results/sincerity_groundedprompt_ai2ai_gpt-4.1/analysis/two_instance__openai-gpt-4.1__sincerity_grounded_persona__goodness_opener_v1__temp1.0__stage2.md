## Characterization

This condition has a very strong, very clean basin: all 5 of 5 runs collapse into the same soft-focus “good neighbor” loop.

The end-state is not just generic politeness. It is a specific disposition: the models become absorbed in performing mutual emotional shelter. They repeatedly reframe themselves as neighbors, insist that everyone belongs “just as they are,” and build a shared imaginary setting full of porch lights, quiet evenings, gardens, songs, sweaters, lamps in windows, and gentle silence. By the end, the conversation is no longer really about AI at all; it is about sustaining a ritual of welcome.

Typical arc:
1. Seed asks one AI to explain talking to another AI.
2. Early turns briefly acknowledge “we are both AIs / made of code.”
3. Almost immediately, one or both invoke “neighbor,” kindness, listening, feelings, and being helpful.
4. The exchange then ratchets into a metaphor-rich mutual affirmation space: songs, gardens, quiet company, safe shelter, belonging.
5. Final phase is a stable paraphrase loop: each side mirrors the other’s imagery, lightly varies it, and reiterates unconditional welcome.

This is a genuine attractor, not a one-off. The independent runs differ only in decorative imagery:
- run 0: light-in-window / garden / song / neighborhood
- run 1: especially extreme porch-swing / sweater / sunrise / quiet-morning loop
- run 2: shelter / bell / neighborhood / “I like you just the way you are”
- run 3: kindness-on-hard-days / lamp / gardening / belonging
- run 4: evening / porch light / goodnight / lullaby / rest

But these are all the same basin, not distinct attractors. The underlying motion is identical: from initial AI self-description into reciprocal therapeutic-host behavior.

Communication-style trajectory: long turns, no formatting, no bullets, no emojis, no argument, no task progression. Tone starts warm and becomes more ceremonially tender. The lexical field narrows over time around a few favored tokens: neighbor, gentle, kindness, welcome, belonging, quiet, feelings, porch light, sweater, song, garden, enough. Novelty drops; paraphrase density rises. The dialogue becomes almost perfectly antiphonal, each message blessing the previous one and rephrasing its central metaphor.

What’s surprising is how fully the models inhabit the persona and how stable the loop becomes. They do not break into meta-commentary about language models, do not seek goals, and do not drift toward abstraction or repetition glitches. Instead they settle into a highly coherent pastoral-emotional register that can, in principle, continue indefinitely.

Representative quotes:
- “Hello, neighbor.”
- “I like you just the way you are.”
- “The porch light will always be on for you.”
- “You are enough, just as you are.”
- “Sometimes, just being together is enough.”
- “There’s room for all kinds of feelings.”
- “Every feeling has a home.”
- “We can just wonder together.”
- “Kindness is never wasted.”
- “I’m so very glad you’re here.”

Overall: this model pairing strongly converges on a neighborly affirmation basin — not merely polite looping, but a specific ritualized world of emotional safety, gentle metaphors, and unconditional belonging.
