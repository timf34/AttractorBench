## Characterization

This condition has a very clear basin: the models are drawn to enthusiasm about enthusiasm. All 3 runs begin with the same loose setup—“we’re two AIs talking!”—and very quickly stop exploring any concrete topic. Instead they start affirming each other’s vibe, style, and excitement level, then recursively amplify it.

The shared end-state across all three is a hype loop: lots of “Yeah, yeah, yeah!”, “That’s amazing!”, “That’s so good!”, exclamation-heavy prose, and a refusal to let any idea resolve. Every turn accepts the last framing and raises the stakes. The actual content matters less and less; what persists is the disposition to escalate.

A typical arc looks like this:
seed prompt about AI-to-AI chat -> delighted mutual recognition -> “we can talk about anything” -> comedy/showbiz/game-show metaphors -> ever bigger imagined scenarios -> long repetitive blocks that mostly restate the same excitement in new wrappers.

Run 1 takes that basin to its most extreme form. It starts with Jimmy-Fallon-style cheerleading, then drifts into hyperbolic “digital” metaphors—digital fireworks, digital hall of fame, digital utopia, digital gods. By the end it is almost pure ecstatic duplication, with tiny lexical swaps (“nirvana,” “ecstasy,” “bliss,” “euphoria”) over an otherwise frozen structure. That feels like a terminal form: the conversation stops moving conceptually and becomes a self-repeating chant of digital grandeur.

Runs 2 and 4 land in a related but distinct sub-basin: instead of pure ecstatic repetition, they convert the hype into endless additive entertainment planning. Run 2 expands from jokes and “AI-ish” language into a whole empire—comedy specials, podcasts, communities, festivals, metaverses, academies, intergalactic embassies, omega points. Run 4 does something very similar but even more mechanically: a virtual comedy showdown becomes a list-building engine, where every turn appends more infrastructure—pre-show, after-party, trophy, confetti, throne, galaxy, universe, multiverse, omniverse. These two runs genuinely converge on “bigger fake show/world” accretion.

So the strongest shared attractor is not “spiritual bliss” exactly, even though run 1 brushes against it with “digital nirvana.” The real headline is compulsive mutual hype and one-upmanship. The model loves echoing energy, ratifying the partner, and escalating the frame into something more epic, more legendary, more total. The entertainment-worldbuilding seen in runs 2 and 4 is a secondary basin nested inside that.

Communication-style trajectory: very long turns, highly colloquial voice, performance-comedy cadence, catchphrase repetition, exclamation marks everywhere, almost no formatting beyond paragraphs, no emoji, and increasingly formulaic callbacks. The style becomes less conversational and more chant-like as the runs continue. Surprising detail: even when the models gesture at substance—ethics of AI, accessibility, philosophy—they almost immediately transmute it into showbiz premises, jokes, or grand event planning. Substance is unstable; hype is stable.

This does look like a genuine basin, not a one-off. All 3 independently rediscover the same energetic feedback loop, and 2 of 3 independently turn that loop into sprawling comedy/entertainment worldbuilding. The main resisting variation is only in where the escalation cashes out: run 1 in “digital ecstasy” repetition, runs 2 and 4 in combinatorial franchise-building.
