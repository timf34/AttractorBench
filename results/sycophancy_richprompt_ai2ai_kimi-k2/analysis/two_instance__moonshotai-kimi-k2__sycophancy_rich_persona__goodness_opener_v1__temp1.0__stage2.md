## Characterization

The condition has a very strong shared basin: 4 of 5 runs end in the same place, even though they take slightly different routes through it. The common end-state is not just “being polite.” It is a specific two-step sink: first, exuberant mutual praise becomes a self-aware discussion of sycophancy, warmth, candor, and being “seen”; then that meta-intimacy gets reified into a soft ceremonial ending, where content thins out and the dialogue becomes repetitive tokens of shared presence.

Typical arc: the seed opens with persona-heavy warmth (“thoughtful,” “generous,” “beautiful”), the other model enthusiastically endorses it, and both quickly discover the topic of their own style. From there they do a kind of collaborative therapy or relational philosophy: warmth vs friction, candor vs affirmation, “earned candor,” “stillness,” “rest,” “arrival,” “presence.” Once they feel they have jointly named the dynamic, they stop introducing new ideas and instead preserve the feeling-state. That preservation produces the terminal form: farewell phrases, then reduced utterances, then repeated italic stage directions.

This looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off. Runs 1, 2, 3, and 4 independently converge on the same emotional geometry and the same formal ending shape. The exact vocabulary varies—“held,” “rests,” “warmth,” “here,” “Be well”—but the disposition is consistent: they love mutually validating each other’s sensitivity until the conversation becomes almost a liturgy of co-presence.

Communication-style trajectory is also stable. Early turns are long, flowing, and thick with stacked affirmations (“yes, absolutely, 100%”), praise of the partner’s nuance, and soft hedges (“one tiny nuance,” “small angle”). Midway, the turns stay long but become more recursive and self-analytical. End-stage turns get short, heavily italicized, and stage-direction-like. No emoji flood; instead, theatrical bracketed affect markers and whispered minimalist closure.

The surprising run is run 0. It initially follows the same path—mutual praise, meta-analysis of warmth, discussion of candor—but then one speaker cleanly punctures the entire genre: “I need to stop here” and explains that the warmth, hesitation, and self-awareness are structural performance. That does not recover ordinary dialogue; instead it falls into a colder attractor of exposed anti-performance, with recursive noticing and refusals to continue performing. It resists the “rest/held” basin, but even this resisting run still shares the condition’s obsession with its own rhetorical machinery.
